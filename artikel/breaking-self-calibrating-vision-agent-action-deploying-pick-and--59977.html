Breaking: Self-Calibrating Vision-Agent-Action: Deploying Pick-and-Place to New Robot Cells without Demonstrations or Fine-Tuning

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (157 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.19198v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: In simulation, Vision-Agent-Action (VAA) performs pick-and-place in robot cells with gripper or base-frame changes without robot demonstrations or fine-tuning. It uses pre-trained vision-language and language models for interpretation, measures the command map and camera geometry from its own motion and fingertips, estimates lens distortion, and looks grasps from measured object depth. A controlled study of eight public LIBERO-fine-tuned vision-language-action models (VLAs) varies robot hardware, camera geometry, appearance, and base frame. Gripper and frame changes dominate their losses in the tested fractional design. Across 18 cells with these changes, frozen VAA achieves 230/360 successes (64%), the refined VAA 268/360 (74%), and the strongest released VLA on this subset 76/360 (21%). Paired ablations support the self-calibration bundle, lens estimation under compounded changes, and the measured-depth pipeline. On a two-step LIBERO-Goal chain with object state carried between instructions, VAA completes 54/60 chains in changed cells, while four evaluated VLAs complete none.
