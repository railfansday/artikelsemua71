Remark on internal relaxation in large number qubit systems

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (146 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.21681v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: For a time $t_{c}$ of calculation in an $N$ qubit quantum computer that is much shorter than the intrinsic Heisenberg time $t_H$, $t_c\ll t_H$, the dynamics of the system is effectively irreversible and the couplings between the qubits give rise to a relaxation time scale $\tau_r$. If $t_H\gg t_c >\sim \tau_r$ one looses quantum coherence exponentially fast. Since $t_H$ scales exponentially with $N$ there is a threshold $N_c$ such that for $N\gg N_c$ one must be aware of possible intrinsic relaxation during calculation. As an order of magnitude estimation we find $N_c\approx \ln(\tau_r/T)$ where $T$ is a typical system specific oscillation time of a single qubit. $\tau_r$ can be estimated as the typical inverse transition rate between two parts of the system, one of which is treated as an environment of the other.
