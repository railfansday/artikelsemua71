Latest Update: CanopyKV: Remember beyond the Crop for Fast Active Visual Reasoning

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (211 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.24373v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: Active visual reasoning enables multimodal models to answer challenging visual questions by gathering fine-grained evidence through iterative image inspection and tool use. However, accumulating crops and reasoning tokens expands the key-value (KV) cache, making compression key for reducing memory and inference costs. Existing KV compression methods typically select historical tokens based on attention, recency, or redundancy. These criteria do not explicitly distinguish locally replaced evidence from complementary information needed for future exploration: each crop refreshes a local region, while earlier views may still support subsequent decisions. Discarding such evidence can impair accuracy or require additional search, while scoring and cache reorganization can offset the computational savings from compression. We introduce CanopyKV, a training-free KV compression framework that uses parent-child crop relationships to retain complementary evidence, keeps retained history stable, and protects the state needed for ongoing reasoning. Tool metadata provides retention signals without additional attention scoring, while compact KV storage reduces subsequent attention costs. Across VisualProbe, V*, HR-Bench, and MME-Lite, CanopyKV achieves state-of-the-art answer accuracy and end-to-end efficiency among the evaluated KV compression methods. On MME-Lite, it retains approximately one-quarter of Dense's total KV while improving accuracy by 10.5 percentage points and accelerating complete reasoning by $1.64\times$.
