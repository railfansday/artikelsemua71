AsymEEF: Bimanual Manipulation with Asymmetric End Effectors

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (172 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.20557v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Teleoperated demonstrations are costly to collect and rarely reusable across end-effectors with different kinematics and action spaces. This burden grows in bimanual manipulation, where each end-effector pair may require new data. We present AsymEEF, a framework for executable cross-end-effector demonstration generation and unified bimanual policy learning. Given a small source dataset, AsymEEF transfers demonstrations to heterogeneous combinations of two-, three-, and five-fingered end-effectors without additional teleoperation. Kinematic teacher anchors provide geometric supervision, while sparse real-world X-Calib anchors correct discrepancies in grasp execution. A shared encoder maps wrist poses and actuator states to a morphology-agnostic latent space, and a morphology-conditioned decoder reconstructs target commands. Generated trajectories undergo replay filtering for task-level executability. The representation also supports a unified bimanual visuomotor policy trained across multiple pairs. Across four simulated and six real-world tasks, AsymEEF improves replay success over adapted transfer baselines, achieves a mean replay-valid expansion gain of 16.6 times in simulation, and is evaluated through downstream policy training on real robots. Project website: https://asymeef.vercel.app/.
