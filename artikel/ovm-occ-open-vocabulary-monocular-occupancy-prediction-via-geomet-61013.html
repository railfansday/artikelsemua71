OVM-Occ: Open-Vocabulary Monocular Occupancy Prediction via Geometry-Aware Vision-Language Distillation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (194 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2610.35312v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Monocular occupancy prediction in indoor scenes aims to recover a scene's 3D geometry and semantics from a single RGB image. Existing methods are restricted to a closed set of predefined categories, limiting their ability to generalize to unseen categories. This limitation is particularly critical in indoor scenes, where objects exhibit highly diverse and fine-grained semantic categories. We study open-vocabulary monocular occupancy prediction for indoor scenes and present OVM-Occ, a Gaussian-based occupancy framework with an instance-aware vision-language teacher. Specifically, we adopt Language-Embedded Gaussians as the intermediate scene representation, propose a geometry-aware deformable attention module to refine Gaussian semantic features, and construct instance-level vision-language embeddings to provide reliable semantic supervision. We further introduce geometry-aware decoupled semantic distillation to mitigate the interference between geometry learning and semantic learning. To evaluate open-vocabulary occupancy prediction performance, we introduce OpenOcc-ScanNet and OpenOcc-Replica, two extended benchmarks with more diverse and fine-grained semantic categories. Experiments show that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in open-vocabulary monocular occupancy prediction for indoor scenes, outperforming the previous state-of-the-art approach by 12.2 mIoU and 0.6 IoU on the Occ-ScanNet dataset, with consistent gains under new open-vocabulary evaluation protocols.
