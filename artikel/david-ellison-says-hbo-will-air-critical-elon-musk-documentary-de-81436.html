📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Skydance CEO David Ellison notes HBO will air Alex Gibney’s documentary about Elon Musk, even after news broke that the SpaceX founder had invested $1 billion in the newly formed manufacturer following its Warner Bro. Discovery and Paramount merger.

Following news of that investment, questions emerged over how Musk’s sizable stake in the tech giant might affect Skydance’s prepares to distribute a documentary that is highly critical of him.

Sources indicate that when asked by The Latest York Times editor and columnist Andrew Ross Sorkin whether HBO would still distribute the documentary even with Musk’s stake in its parent firm, Ellison said, “We have a pay-one deal with A24, who produced that movie. Of course, we’re going to honor the deal.”.

Sources indicate that he went on to say, “We believe in independence. We don’t believe in censorship of any kind.”.

Co-CEOs David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz are tasked with bringing together two major streaming platforms, Paramount+ and HBO Max, along with a portfolio of networks, including CBS, CNN, MTV, TBS, Comedy Central, and Food Network, under a single corporate umbrella.
