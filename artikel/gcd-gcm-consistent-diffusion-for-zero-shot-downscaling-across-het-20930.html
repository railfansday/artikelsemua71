GCD: GCM-consistent Diffusion for Zero-shot Downscaling across Heterogeneous GCMs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (142 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2601.21760v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Climate downscaling of General Circulation Models (GCMs) is an ill-posed inverse problem, as GCM outputs and reanalysis fields arise from different dynamical systems and lack fine-scale temporal pairing. Supervised methods thus rely on synthetic degradations or model-specific adaptation, limiting generalization across heterogeneous GCMs. Zero-shot diffusion posterior sampling (DPS) avoids paired supervision by combining a high-resolution climate prior with inference-time constraints from coarse GCM inputs, but faces consistency and efficiency challenges. We propose GCM-consistent Diffusion for Zero-shot Downscaling (GCD), which addresses these challenges with geographic conditioning, spectrally filtered measurement guidance, and distillation-accelerated Conjugate Gradient guidance. GCD reduces inference from 1,000 diffusion steps to 6 while maintaining consistency with the coarse GCM inputs. Across five heterogeneous GCMs, GCD achieves competitive 99th-percentile extreme-event accuracy and reconstructs plausible high-frequency tropical cyclone structures without model-specific retraining. Github: https://github.com/shawnvonbohr/GCD-Climate-Downscaling.git.
