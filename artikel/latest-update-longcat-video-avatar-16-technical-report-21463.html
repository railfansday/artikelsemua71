Latest Update: LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6 Technical Report

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (155 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.18822v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Recent advances in audio-driven avatar video generation have enabled increasingly realistic synthesis, yet achieving precise lip-audio synchronization, natural, human-like motion, and visual consistency over long sequences remains challenging. We present LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6, which builds on LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5 to improve expressive naturalness and reduce error accumulation during extended generation, e.g., exaggerated lip movements and overly red, temporally unstable lip coloration, enhance the naturalness of facial expressions and coordinated head and body movements. These improvements are driven by a refined post-training pipeline that integrates reinforcement learning, improved classifier-free guidance strategies for DMD-based few-step distillation, long-video rollouts, and on-policy distillation. Together, these refinements improve expressive realism and enable the generation of videos exceeding 10 minutes while maintaining visual quality and temporal consistency. Arena evaluations show that LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6 outperforms its predecessor and competing commercial audio-driven avatar models, demonstrating the effectiveness of the improved post-training pipeline.
