Industry News: Analysis of the Second-Harmonic Generation Process in Nonlinear Metasurfaces Based on Multiple Quantum Wells: The Approach of Nonlinear Current Density, and The Development of Boundary Conditions

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (238 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.23943v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Using plasmonic metasurfaces integrated with nonlinear materials, such as multiple quantum wells (MQWs), has been an excellent solution to enhance nonlinear light-matter interactions. In this approach, a plasmonic resonator enhances the intensity of the fundamental harmonic within the MQWs region, increasing the strength of the intersubband transitions inside the MQWs. As a result, the efficiency of second harmonic generation (SHG) increases significantly. In this paper, we present a newly introduced approach to analyze the SHG process in nonlinear metasurfaces based on MQWs. In this approach, first, we formulate the nonlinear current density within the MQW region, which serves as the radiation source of the second harmonic wave. Notably, the saturation effect in MQWs, that limits the nonlinear response, is accounted for. Second, we address the development of boundary conditions for the second harmonic (SH) field in periodic structures, which ultimately leads us to define periodic boundary conditions for the SH field. Third, we combine these elements into a semi-analytical framework and validate it against previously reported experimental data. Finally, an efficient MQWs structure and plasmonic metasurface are designed for the fundamental wavelength of 8 $\mu$m. After implementing the proposed algorithm, we observe that the designed nonlinear metasurface achieves a significant conversion efficiency of 0.02% under the incident intensity of 23.5 kW/cm$^2$. KEYWORDS: Second harmonic generation, metasurface, multiple quantum wells, nonlinear current density, periodic boundary conditions.
