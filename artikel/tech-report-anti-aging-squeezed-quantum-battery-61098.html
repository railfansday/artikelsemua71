Tech Report: Anti-aging Squeezed Quantum Battery

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (162 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22452v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Exhibiting the superiority of energy storage and conversion over its classical counterpart, quantum battery (QB) emerges as a promising platform to revolutionize traditional energy technologies. However, their practical performance is severely constrained by the ubiquitous decoherence in microscopic world, which causes rapid aging and reduced ergotropy. Here, we propose a decoherence-tolerant QB consisting of a continuous-variable bosonic mode. Acting as the quantum resource, the utilized squeezing endows the QB with unprecedented energy capacity and ergotropy. Through investigating the non-Markovian decoherence effect on the QB, we find that such squeezing enhanced performance can be persistently preserved under the influence of decoherence as long as a bound state is present in the energy spectrum of the total system formed by the QB and the environment. This decoherence-tolerance feature is even maintained under moderate finite-temperature condition. Providing a useful energy storage paradigm for designing long-lived QBs, our result sheds light on the realization of anti-aging QBs.
