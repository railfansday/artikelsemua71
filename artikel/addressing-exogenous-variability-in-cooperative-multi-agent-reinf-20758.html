Addressing Exogenous Variability in Cooperative Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (183 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.22067v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) often fails under exogenous variability such as shifts in opponents or environment regimes that cannot be controlled by the team but alter the transition dynamics. We formalize this challenge as Exogenous Dec-POMDP (ED-POMDP), in which an episode-level exogenous regime, selected independently of the team, indexes the dynamics, and the global state is partitioned into components that are directly responsive to the current team action and components that are not directly action-responsive at the current step. This formulation exposes mediated influence: while team actions do not directly upgrade the latter at the current step, they can shape their later evolution through induced changes in the former. Based on this, we propose LEICA, a CTDE-compatible algorithm that learns history-conditioned context representations for both components and shapes policy updates using agent-wise, influence-weighted counterfactual intrinsic rewards. Across SMAX benchmarks with opponent strategy shifts, LEICA consistently improves both training performance and generalization to unseen opponents' strategies over existing baselines, supporting the usefulness of influence-weighted shaping under exogenous train-test regime shifts.
