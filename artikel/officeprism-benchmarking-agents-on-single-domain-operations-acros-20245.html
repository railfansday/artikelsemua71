OfficePrism: Benchmarking Agents on Single-Domain Operations Across Enterprise Business Functions

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (175 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22262v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Broad workplace scores can mix competence within one business function with the extra cost of coordinating across functions and systems, making failures hard to localize. OfficePrism evaluates this prerequisite through 420 single-domain enterprise tasks spanning seven business functions under shared, benchmark-defined L1--L3 task-structure levels. Each task runs in a fresh environment combining a shared synthetic organization with one function-specific domain and yped state access. Our pipeline uses grounded records, business rules, and tool schemas to jointly construct a Chinese-language ticket, a hidden executable reference witness, and an expected response, followed by automated filtering and human curation. The same task set is evaluated through full-schema and search-and-dispatch packages. Across 124 model--harness--package configurations, the per-case joint natural-language-and-database pass rate ranges from 22.6% to 76.4%, with a median of 67.9%. L1 is highest in all 124 configurations, and L2 exceeds L3 in 122. Among 50 configurations sharing the same harness and model labels across both packages, search scores higher in 30, ties in one, and scores lower in 19.
