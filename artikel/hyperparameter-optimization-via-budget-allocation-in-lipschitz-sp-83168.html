Hyperparameter Optimization via Budget Allocation in Lipschitz Spaces

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (139 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2510.10605v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Hyperparameter optimization and model selection often require allocating a limited evaluation budget across many candidate configurations, where performance improves with additional training or resources. We study this setting when each configuration has an unknown performance curve over budget. Similarity between configurations induces similarity between their curves, capturing geometric structure in the search space. We formalize the resulting decision problem as Unknown Value Probing and propose budget allocation strategies that combine principled exploration of the configuration space with value-informed concentration on regions supported by observed evidence. We prove worst-case approximation guarantees for our approach and show these guarantees are near-optimal by matching corresponding hardness barriers. Extensive experiments on large benchmark suites spanning more than 250 experimental settings demonstrate consistent gains over strong baselines in both anytime behavior and final incumbent performance.
