Latest Update: Superspecial curves of genus two over prime fields: Frobenius types and rational (2,2)-kernels

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (310 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.23694v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Superspecial curves of genus two over a prime field $\mathbb{F}_p$, and the rational $(2,2)$-isogenies between their Jacobians, are the vertices and edges of the graph on which genus-two isogeny-based cryptography is built. Let $C$ be such a curve, with Jacobian $J$ and Frobenius endomorphism $\pi$. We show that $\pi^2 = p\,\alpha_*$ for a unique automorphism $\alpha$ of $C$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$ whose action on the Weierstrass points is the square of that of Frobenius. For $p \ge 7$ this gives the list of Frobenius types of the Weierstrass points: eight types, each with its Weil polynomial, a congruence condition on $p$, and its number 15, 3, 3, 7, 1, 1, 3 or 1 of $\mathbb{F}_p$-rational $(2,2)$-kernels; when all Weierstrass points are rational, the 2-Tate pairing on $J[2]$ is determined by $p \bmod 8$. We then prove closed formulas, in terms of class numbers of imaginary quadratic orders and of $\zeta_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt p)}(-1)$, for the mass of the curves of each type and for the mass of the rational kernels whose quotient is a product of elliptic curves. For $\pi^2 = \pm p$ the total masses are due to Ibukiyama and to Xue and Yu; our proofs use the 2-adic Tate modules, the symmetry of the Richelot correspondence and Eichler's mass formula, and, for the other curves, modules over orders of a quartic CM field. The kernels are counted through gluings of supersingular elliptic curves. As a consequence, the proportion of rational kernels with decomposed quotient is of order $p^{-1/2}(1+L(1,\chi))$, where $\chi$ is the quadratic character of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-p})$. All formulas agree with an exact enumeration for the 37 primes $7 \le p \le 173$. Finally we describe the graph of $\mathbb{F}_p$-rational Richelot isogenies, which is far from connected in the range computed, and discuss what these results say about its use in isogeny-based cryptography.
