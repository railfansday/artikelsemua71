The Illusion of Relief: How GenAI Redistributes Cognitive Workload in Software Organizations

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (115 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.23286v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: This study explores how Generative AI (GenAI) reshapes software practitioners' cognitive work across software development activities. We analyzed questionnaire responses from 44 practitioners across nine organizations using descriptive quantitative methods and qualitative analysis of relevant open-ended responses. Practitioners reported productivity and time savings, but also effort associated with providing context, prompting, reviewing, correcting, verifying, and integrating AI-generated outputs. Human judgment and accountability remained central, with practitioners anticipating a shift from direct code production to guiding, evaluating, validating, integrating, and coordinating AI-supported work. Thus, GenAI appears to redistribute cognitive work toward oversight rather than simply eliminate it, although whether overall cognitive workload increases or decreases remains undetermined.
