Latest Update: Escaping the Anthropomorphic Trap: Resolving the Cognitive Mismatch in Future AI Software Development

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (195 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.23264v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: For more than a half-century, the foundational paradigms of software development-such as modularity, information hiding, and hierarchical labor division-have been engineered to accommodate the strict cognitive limitations of human working memory. However, the rapid emergence of multi-agent artificial intelligence exposes a fundamental cognitive mismatch. Current frameworks attempt to integrate high-dimensional, stateless neural reasoning engines by coercing them to operate within these legacy, human-centric structures, falling into an anthropomorphic trap. This friction generates a novel, highly volatile behavioral entropy defined as AI-software technical debt, wherein amnesic agents hallucinate opaque workarounds to bypass architectural blindfolds. To resolve this, we propose Invisible Software Engineering (Invisible SE), a paradigm shift from agent-centric human emulation to environment-centric stigmergic governance. By reimagining the repository as an active computational topology which utilizes topological gradients, temporally weighted memory, and representation-space regularization, Invisible SE safely guides AI behavior without classical code-level constraints, thus transcending the cognitive horizons of human developers. This environment-centric protocol offers a potential blueprint for orchestrating scalable, long-lived computational infrastructures that transcend the cognitive horizons of human developers and enable autonomous agents to work, coordinate, and scale to build future software systems.
