Tech Report: EasyNav: A Modular Architecture for Real-Time Navigation Across Heterogeneous Robot Environments

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (170 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.19894v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Most robot navigation stacks implicitly bind mapping, localization, planning, and control to specific representations and data flows, yielding architectures hard to extend, compare experimentally, or reason about end-to-end timing. This is critical in heterogeneous scenarios, where multiple spatial representations, sensing modalities, and coordinate frames coexist and evolve at different rates. This paper presents a navigation architecture centered on an explicit, shared navigation state that serves as the sole interaction interface between modular components. Constraining mapping, localization, planning, and control to operate over this shared state enables interchangeable, representation-agnostic pipelines under explicit compatibility and timing contracts. We realize this architecture as EasyNav, an open source ROS 2 framework, and validate it through real-robot experiments, including indoor navigation with laser sensing and large-scale indoor-outdoor navigation with GPS and 3D LiDAR across multi-level routes. Compared with Nav2 under matched conditions, EasyNav reduces reaction latency from 377 to 33 ms indoors and from 71 to 8 ms outdoors, while also using less CPU and memory.
