Industry News: Local distinguishability of five six-partite orthogonal product states

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (134 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.22553v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: The local distinguishability of a set of orthogonal quantum states can be used to reduce the amount of quantum state transmission in quantum protocols and lower system operational costs and quantum resource overhead. In this paper, we investigate the local distinguishability of five six-partite orthogonal product states (OPSs), where any two states are orthogonal on only one subsystem. We classify all feasible sets of five six-partite OPSs into five categories with 249 cases in total, and prove that these sets can be perfectly distinguished by local operations and classical communication, except for two cases. For five six-partite OPSs corresponding to these two exceptional cases, we further discuss their local distinguishability. Our work fully characterizes the local distinguishability of all feasible sets of five six-partite OPSs.
