Breaking: WinDEX: Metric 3D Hand Motion Reconstruction in the Wild for Dexterous Teleoperation and Policy Learning

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (196 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.21143v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Human manipulation data are widely used to train robots for human-like dexterity. However, low-cost, large-scale collection in the wild remains challenging. Existing methods remain limited in geometrically grounded metric-scale reconstruction, real-world generalization and inference speed. We present WinDEX, integrating (1) geometric-temporal optimization for metric 3D hand annotation from in-the-wild egocentric stereo videos, combined with visual-inertial SLAM for world-frame reconstruction; (2) WinDEX-Net, an end-to-end, fisheye-native network combining calibrated stereo, local detail and temporal context, with a data-model flywheel of model-assisted annotation and retraining; (3) distilled models for offline reconstruction and causal streaming, supporting reinforcement learning and teleoperation; and (4) low-cost CyberCap stereo-inertial capture, forming the WinDEX-Hand dataset with 1,000 hours of training annotations and held-out human-annotated in-the-wild 2D and independent optical motion-capture evaluation sets. Offline reconstruction runs at 43.61 FPS and causal streaming at 171 FPS. On held-out data, offline reconstruction achieves state-of-the-art accuracy: 14.5 pixels in-the-wild reprojection error and, with visual-inertial odometry, 37.29 mm world-frame joint error. We achieve 7.6 and 3.6 times the task success rates of reinforcement learning policies trained with WiLoR and HaWoR motion references, respectively, and demonstrate real-time arm-hand teleoperation. Project website: https://fruitrabbit.github.io/WinDEX/.
