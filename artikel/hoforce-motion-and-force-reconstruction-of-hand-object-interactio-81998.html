HOForce: Motion and Force Reconstruction of Hand-Object Interaction Using a Single-View RGB-D Video

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (149 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.23871v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Reconstructing hand-object interactions is an important task, and achieving it with a single camera makes large-scale capture practical but challenging due to heavy occlusions. We introduce HOForce to track hands and objects, and further recover interaction forces from single-view RGB-D video and known object properties. For two-hand manipulation, we jointly model fingertip forces of both hands and the contact forces between objects when two objects are presented. The reconstructed forces also improve motion tracking by giving pose constraints in the occluded regions. HOForce recovers hand and object motion more accurately on DexYCB and TACO and produces less hand-object penetration than interaction baselines on DexYCB. On our dataset of fingertip forces recorded with tactile sensors, HOForce reduces normal-force MAE by 24.5% relative to the strongest baseline. The recovered forces reproduce object motion in MuJoCo and transfer to other engines after adaptation.
