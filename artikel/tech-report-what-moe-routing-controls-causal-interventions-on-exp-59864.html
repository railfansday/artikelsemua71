Tech Report: What MoE Routing Controls: Causal Interventions on Expert Trajectories

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (221 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.22120v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In mixture-of-experts (MoE) language models, a token is routed to a sequence of experts across layers. We investigate how forcing tokens onto chosen expert paths influences model behavior. We build a target path from the experts that a concept's or role's tokens most often use, and we add a fixed bias to each target expert's router logit during generation to steer the token toward this expert path. We study two kinds of targets. Format targets concern the form of the output, and concept targets concern its topic. In Qwen3-30B-A3B, we show that steering a format path changes the text the model writes. We also show that steering a concept path shifts the model's next-token probabilities toward the concept, even when the path is built in another language, yet this shift has little effect on the generated text. We investigate why formats reach the text but concepts do not by steering only part of the generation, and find evidence that a format, once started, reinforces itself. We show that the concepts religion and money steer most strongly, and that the effect is concentrated in the middle layers. We attempt to explain why the strength of the effect varies by concept through an analysis of embedding distinctiveness and word frequency.
