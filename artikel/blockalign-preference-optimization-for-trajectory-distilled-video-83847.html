BlockAlign: Preference Optimization for Trajectory-Distilled Video Generation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (241 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.20351v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Directly aligning trajectory-distilled video generators slashes rollout costs and optimizes the deployed few-step model without teacher fine-tuning or re-distillation. However, conventional flow-matching preference objectives assume single-head local velocity supervision, mismatching the finite-interval, block-average transition interface of trajectory-distilled generators such as Parallel Decoding Distillation (PDD) and Parametric Trajectory Distillation (PTD). Naively supervising individual unfused sub-interval velocity heads across all sub-steps rather than the fused block-average velocity at block starts (DirectAlign) induces a severe training--inference mismatch that degrades preference below the unaligned baseline. We introduce BlockAlign, an interface-consistent preference optimization framework that constructs states at valid block boundaries, queries the fused block-level velocity predictor, and supervises span-normalized transition residuals---seamlessly supporting DPO-, NFT-, and rollout-style objectives while preserving the native few-step sampler. We further build a unified post-training pipeline integrating offline pairs, reward-free semi-online continuations, and online rollouts with hierarchical preference selection and objective-aware block scheduling. Experiments on multi-reference video generation across MiniMax-H3 Ref2VA and an in-house model RefVid-V4 show that BlockAlign consistently outperforms unaligned distilled models and DirectAlign in structural plausibility, reference consistency, and visual quality. Notably, our approach unlocks PDD's 5x few-step rollout speedup for 3x faster end-to-end online training, achieving statistically significant human preference gains over PDD baselines (54.0% vs. 33.3% wins/losses, or 61.8% non-tie win rate on RefVid-V4; 51.3% vs. 34.7%, or 59.7% non-tie win rate on MiniMax-H3) and up to an 82.7% win rate against DirectAlign.
