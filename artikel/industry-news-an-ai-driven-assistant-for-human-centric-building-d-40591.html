Industry News: An AI-Driven Assistant for Human-Centric Building Digital Twin Systems: Design, Implementation, and Comparative User Evaluation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (159 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22350v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Smart buildings generate streams of operational data, yet this richness rarely translates into accessible, decision-ready information for stakeholders who inhabit and manage them. Current Building Digital Twin Systems (BDTS) often remain oriented toward technical specialists and rely on static, one-size-fits-all interfaces. This paper presents the design, implementation, and comparative user evaluation of an AI-driven conversational assistant integrated into a human-centric BDTS for Indoor Environmental Quality monitoring, combined with adaptive information presentation and auto-suggest functionality. The prototype was deployed at Upcoming York University Abu Dhabi. A controlled, two-arm, between-subjects, quasi-experimental study with wave-based, non-randomized assignment compared the Baseline interface with the Experimental interface. Results indicated significant reductions in task-completion time and perceived workload, while accuracy and usability improved but did not meet the adjusted significance criterion. The study contributes an implemented and empirically evaluated architecture that separates generative interaction from deterministic environmental assessment and coordinates conversational responses with adaptive visual presentation.
