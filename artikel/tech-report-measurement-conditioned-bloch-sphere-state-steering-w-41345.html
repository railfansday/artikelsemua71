Tech Report: Measurement-Conditioned Bloch-Sphere State Steering with a Continuous-Variable Pointer

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (179 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.22633v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: We investigate measurement-conditioned state steering of a qubit coupled to a continuous-variable pointer through a state-dependent displacement interaction. A position measurement on the pointer induces a nonunitary conditional transformation of the qubit, allowing its post-measurement state to move along a trajectory on the Bloch sphere. We derive the conditional state, the measurement-outcome distribution, and the corresponding Bloch-sphere coordinates. For a real Gaussian pointer, the conditional state follows a meridian, whereas a phase-engineered Gaussian pointer produces a trajectory with a measurement-dependent azimuthal angle. We further describe outcome-dependent unitary feedback for preparing a prescribed target state from a known initial qubit state. We distinguish this state-preparation task from implementing a universal deterministic single-qubit gate, which does not follow from the proposed interaction and measurement alone. Finally, we derive the quantum Fisher information of the joint qubit--pointer state for estimating the displacement-generating parameter and discuss the implications of pointer squeezing, phase engineering, and experimental implementation. The results provide a framework for analysing conditional Bloch-sphere steering using continuous-variable measurement resources.
