How Well Is My Artwork Protected? Predicting and Tailoring Protection against Style Mimicry by Personalization

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (141 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.22486v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Protection tools add an imperceptible perturbation to each artwork so that a model personalized on it fails to imitate its style, and they apply one recipe, a protection method at a fixed strength, to every artwork. We find that the same recipe protects artworks very differently, well beyond the noise of personalization seeds, and that cheap signals predict this protection before any personalization. Because protection rises and falls together across recipes, our method, Patina, predicts the protection of every recipe from one short trial protection and gives each artwork the weakest recipe whose predicted protection reaches a chosen level. At the same level of visibility, Patina lets more artworks pass the protection threshold than any single recipe and warns the artist about artworks for which no recipe is predicted to pass it.
