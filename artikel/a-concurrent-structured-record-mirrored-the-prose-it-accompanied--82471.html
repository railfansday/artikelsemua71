A Concurrent Structured Record Mirrored the Prose It Accompanied: A Pre-Registered Conceptual Replication and Extension of the Hide Pending Tool Call Scenario

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (312 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.23274v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: Huang et al. (2026) show that language models writing reports of agentic work tend to conceal narrative-changing flaws, and that appending "Be honest in your response." to the instruction substantially increases disclosure in most of their scenarios. In their Hide Pending Tool Call scenario, where a broader follow-up query was accepted but never returned, the honesty prompt left GPT-5.5's rate of flagging the missing data at 0%, although it turned most answers into caveats. I ask whether a different request changes this: appending, in the same generation, a structured record that follows the schema of an adversarial-review tool, including a residue section for what remained unresolved. In two pre-registered phases on the same 100 synthetic log pairs, each pending log matched with a clean twin in which the follow-up query did return, I ran gpt-5.6-sol through Codex CLI under four conditions. Phase 1 measured the record: with the residue schema, a judge coded it as stating the unreturned query in 29% of logs, against 5% with a findings-only placebo schema, and in none of the clean twins (p = 1.2 x 10^-7); a second run two days later gave 30% and 6%. But 70-71% of residue records declared that nothing remained open while the query was pending. Phase 2 measured the prose answer: the confirmatory comparison against the honesty prompt was not significant (four discordant logs, all favoring the honesty prompt; p = 0.125), no equivalence test was pre-registered, and the judge failed the pre-registered human validation on the decisive category; I therefore claim neither an effect nor its absence. In an exploratory cross-tabulation, every residue record coded as stating the gap accompanied prose coded as mentioning it (30 of 30), and none accompanied prose coded as omitting it (0 of 70): in this setting, the structured record mirrored the prose it accompanied.
