Industry News: RadMatch: Auditable Radiology Report Evaluation via Finding-Level Matching

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (220 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2609.01470v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: As AI systems are increasingly used to draft radiology reports, reliably evaluating their clinical quality remains a critical challenge. We introduce RadMatch, a multi-stage LLM-based framework that turns report evaluation into a structured, auditable finding-level record. It extracts findings from a reference and a candidate report, matches them by clinical equivalence, grades each match over ten clinical attributes, and gives every match and unmatched finding a verdict and a clinical-significance tier. To validate metrics on CT, we build and release MetricEval-BodyCT, the first public metric-evaluation benchmark of chest and abdomen CT reports annotated by radiologists error by error. On it and three chest X-ray benchmarks, RadMatch with an open 31B judge on consumer GPUs agrees with radiologists more closely than previous published metrics, and even reaches inter-expert agreement on ReXVal and on the significant errors of MetricEval-BodyCT. Radiologists auditing its decisions on 60 studies confirm 98.5% of its matches and 93.6% of the errors it counts. When used to evaluate state-of-the-art radiology report generation models, RadMatch highlights that most CT models, unlike several chest X-ray models, actually leave more significant errors than an image-blind normal template, as their added unsupported findings outnumber the reference findings they correctly report. We provide RadMatch's code, its dashboard, the MetricEval-BodyCT dataset and all per-report scores.
