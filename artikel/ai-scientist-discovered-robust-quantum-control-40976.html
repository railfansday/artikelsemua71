AI Scientist Discovered Robust Quantum Control

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (190 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.21765v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Protecting quantum coherence is a central theme of modern quantum technologies. Carefully designed control pulses can extend coherence by suppressing disorder and unwanted interactions. However, designing robust pulse sequences remains challenging in interacting multi-level systems, where multiple independent error channels must be controlled in the presence of experimental imperfections. Here we introduce QORA, a multi-agent artificial intelligence framework that integrates theoretical reasoning, automated experimental calibration and control for closed-loop discovery of quantum control sequences. Applied to a dense ensemble of spin-1 nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond, QORA discovers AID-ROD, a series of AI-discovered sequences that nearly double the measured spin coherence time relative to the state-of-the-art human-designed sequence DROID-C3PO. Subsequence analysis by human researchers reveals a hierarchy of error suppression: 18-pulse blocks suppress linear and quadratic onsite disorder and common-mode drive-amplitude errors, while their arrangement over a longer cycle reduces dipolar interactions, providing a physical explanation for the observed improvement and robustness in AID-ROD. These results demonstrate AI-guided experimental discovery of robust quantum control and identify a design strategy for protecting coherence in interacting multi-level systems beyond qubits.
