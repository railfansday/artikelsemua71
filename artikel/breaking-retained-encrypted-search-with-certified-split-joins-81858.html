🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (187 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.24265v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: Retaining encrypted search across data edits and worker replacement requires separating computational validity from current release authority. We give exact Hamming search with at most one authorized decryption and decoding attempt per query preparation. Under authenticated prescribed execution, exclusive nonrollback owner state, an ideal public seed-expansion interface and a stated observation model, we reduce bounded adaptive privacy to ordinary ring learning with errors (Ring-LWE). Expanded second components depend only on public masks and fixed maps, independently of queries and the fresh database key. This compiler invariant permits a retained split join against a smaller-modulus encrypted database. A simultaneous bound on the completed circuit covers a finite honest reservation family, including adaptive queries and slot selection, with explicit statistical correctness and privacy losses. In three post-calibration local CPU comparisons on 32,768 vectors of 512 bits, the 60-bit-join construction takes a median 9.825 s for an end-to-end two-query workload, versus 12.159 s for the optimized full-source-modulus baseline. These times include setup, transfers, preparation and private completion. They are local implementation results, not deployment or concrete-security claims.
