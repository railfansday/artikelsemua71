Analytical prediction of superradiant burst dynamics in extended atomic ensembles

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (168 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22448v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present an open-system quantum theory of forward superradiant bursts in extended two-level atomic ensembles using coupled Heisenberg-Langevin and Maxwell propagation equations. Starting from a fully inverted state with zero mean polarization, local dipole correlations initiate emission without a prescribed tipping angle or stochastic seed. Assuming a spatially uniform population inversion while retaining longitudinal field propagation and spatially dependent atomic polarization, we derive an analytical expression for the absolute photon emission rate into the selected forward optical channel. Coupled to self-consistent inversion dynamics, the expression quantitatively reproduces the pulse waveform and absolute peak rate from spatially resolved stochastic simulations for the sample parameters considered, up to an optical depth (OD) of approximately 200. At higher OD, longitudinal inversion gradients limit the approximation, while the peak rate trends toward Dicke-like quadratic scaling and the pulse width decreases approximately inversely with OD. The framework thus connects quantum initiation, propagation, and inversion depletion while delineating the limits of a spatially reduced description.
