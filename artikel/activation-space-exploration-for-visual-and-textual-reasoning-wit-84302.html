Activation-Space Exploration for Visual and Textual Reasoning with RLVR

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (178 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2610.27012v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) improves visual and textual reasoning by sampling responses, scoring them with an automatic verifier, and updating the policy from the resulting rewards. The policy therefore receives direct learning signals only from trajectories that appear in its rollouts. Yet repeated sampling from the same policy can concentrate on similar reasoning paths, leaving useful alternatives unexplored under a fixed rollout budget. We introduce Activation-Space Exploration (ASE), an online method for diversifying training rollouts. By clustering activation differences between correct and incorrect responses to the same problem, ASE builds a structured bank of latent exploration vectors. Different vectors are then applied to subsequent rollouts to explore alternative reasoning paths. Under the same rollout budget, ASE improves average accuracy over GRPO on Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct by 5.41 and 3.15 percentage points across six visual mathematical and six general visual reasoning benchmarks, respectively. On Qwen3-1.7B-Base, it improves average Mean@8 over GRPO across six mathematical reasoning benchmarks by 4.87 percentage points, with gains also observed on Qwen3-8B-Base.
