ReCALM: A Residual-enhanced Crowdsourced Analytic Learning Mechanism for Distributed Continual Service Construction

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (210 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.19276v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Crowdsourcing has emerged as a promising paradigm for constructing AI services by harnessing the collective capabilities of distributed workers. As consumers' demands continually evolve, service models must acquire new knowledge while retaining previously learned information. However, due to the inherited reliance on gradients for backpropagation, existing approaches typically suffer from catastrophic forgetting during continual model updates on heterogeneous data. Recent studies have introduced analytic learning, a gradient-free paradigm that achieves invariance through recursive closed-form solutions. Nevertheless, existing analytic methods are typically constrained by single-layer linear networks, which tend to suffer from underfitting due to their inability to perform deep representation learning. To address this issue, we propose ReCALM, a Residual-enhanced Crowdsourced Analytic Learning Mechanism that achieves deep nonlinear invariance for distributed continual service construction. Inspired by the success of ResNet, we develop gradient-free residual blocks for ReCALM with closed-form solutions. To implement distributed continual training for ReCALM, we further devise a cross-recursive strategy via least squares. Both theoretical and experimental analyses verify ReCALM's dual superiority in heterogeneity invariance and deep representation. With 20 residual layers, ReCALM improves accuracy by 8.85%-9.83% over the single-layer analytic baseline, while reducing computational costs by over 94% compared with gradient-based baselines.
