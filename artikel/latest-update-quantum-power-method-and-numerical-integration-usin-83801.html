Latest Update: Quantum Power Method and Numerical Integration Using Quantum Singular Value Transformation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (168 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2407.11744v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Quantum singular value transformation (QSVT) is a framework that has been shown to unify many primitives in quantum algorithms. In this work, we leverage the QSVT framework in two directions. We first revisit one recently introduced quantum power method within the QSVT framework and implement its matrix powers with block encodings, which makes the cost of this subroutine polylogarithmic in the inverse precision; the overall running time, however, remains exponential in the number of power iterations because of post-selection. Additionally, we incorporate several elementary numerical integration techniques, such as the rectangular method, Monte Carlo method, and quadrature method, into the QSVT framework, which results in polynomial speedup with respect to the size or the number of points of the grid for the rectangular and quadrature rules, compared with exact classical summation over the grid. Our results thus provide further examples to demonstrate the potential of the QSVT and how it may enhance quantum algorithmic tasks.
