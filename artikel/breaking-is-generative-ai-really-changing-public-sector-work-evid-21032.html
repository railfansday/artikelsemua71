Breaking: Is generative AI really changing public sector work? Evidence from a multi-country survey

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (173 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.16102v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Artificial intelligence has the potential to transform how public services are delivered, with key implications for public service productivity. But how widely is the technology actually being used - and is it making any difference? Our survey of 3,362 public sector workers across the UK, the USA, South Africa and the EU finds widespread use of AI in every region. Our findings suggest that this spread is being driven by bottom-up individual adoption rather than top-down institutional adoption. In the EU, for example, 65% of respondents use AI, while only 19% say their organisation has formally adopted it. Most users are positive about its effects, with 79% saying AI has improved their productivity. However, only 29% say it has increased what is expected of them at work, raising questions about how truly transformative the technology is so far. Across regions, South Africa is a clear outlier, with the highest levels of use, organisational adoption, optimism and awareness of guidelines.
