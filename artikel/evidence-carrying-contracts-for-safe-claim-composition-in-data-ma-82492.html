Evidence-Carrying Contracts for Safe Claim Composition in Data Marketplace Workflows

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (210 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.23276v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Data marketplace workflows cross organizational boundaries, where payment, policy presence, or API delivery may be promoted into transaction claims that the evidence does not support. We introduce evidence-carrying claim contracts: a lightweight assume-guarantee semantics declaring assumptions, guarantees, blocking obligations, and evidence dependencies. A conflict-sensitive four-state domain distinguishes supported, refuted, unresolved, and conflicting requirements. Safe composition bounds the transaction claim by the meet of local bounds and blocks full promotion while a critical obligation remains open. We establish local soundness, compositional boundedness, revocation safety, termination, and aggregation-order invariance for finite acyclic workflows. An executable conformance harness covers all 4,096 configurations (four states for each of six stages), 18 mutations, six basic revocations, 12 alternative-support scenarios, an eight-transition evidence trace, 2,880 aggregation schedules, 96 requirement-level and six interface checks. An independent operational evaluator agrees with the declarative oracle; mutation-sensitive campaigns detect all eight seeded defects, though no single campaign detects all. It problems no unsupported full claim, preserves a full claim under valid alternative support, and blocks it when none remains. Four diagnostic negative controls, not competitive baselines, matter 63-1,023 unsupported full claims. These results establish executable conformance to declared contracts, not specification completeness, evidence authenticity, or legal validity.
