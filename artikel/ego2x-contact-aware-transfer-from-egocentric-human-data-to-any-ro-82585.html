Ego2X: Contact-aware Transfer from Egocentric Human Data to Any Robot

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (231 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24138v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Embodied models depend on real interaction data, yet collecting it on physical robots is slow and labor-intensive. Recent ego-to-robot pipelines retarget first-person view human videos to robot data via transferring hand and arm motion. They judge fidelity mainly by visual appearance. However, trajectories often fail in terms of physics: fingers hover beside objects, and grippers close on empty space. We argue that how the object moves and the contacts that drive the motion count. Thus, we present Ego2X, an object-centric framework that converts monocular egocentric video into physically executable robot data across dexterous hands and parallel grippers. Ego2X centers on CORE (Contact-centric Object-interaction REpresentation), which records each contact on the object by its surface anchor, as well as relative force, grasp mode, and interaction state. Throughout the pipeline, CORE corrects hand-object drift via object-relative constraints, steers inverse kinematics toward contacts the robot must realize, and preserves held objects during arm erasure and robot compositing. On HOT3D, contact-driven correction reduces in-contact hand depth error by 33.7%, while on EgoDex, contact-driven retargeting realizes 62.9% of annotated contacts, surpassing the official HuRo pipeline (27.9%). In RoboTwin 2.0, post-training X-VLA on Ego2X episodes alone, without simulator demonstrations, raises task success rate from 40.5% zero-shot to 67.0% across 200 paired scenes. We will publicly release our conversion pipeline and CORE-annotated dataset for dexterous hands and parallel grippers.
