Industry News: Freeforward4d: Annotation-free feed-forward 4d reconstruction of driving scenes

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (225 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.24007v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Feed-forward 4D reconstruction enables efficient creation of renderable driving scenes for autonomous driving simulation. However, most existing methods rely heavily on dataset-provided cameras, LiDAR depth, or 3D box annotations, limiting their applicability to unannotated driving videos. Pretrained visual models can predict cameras, depth, and object tracks from RGB alone, but their geometry is mutually inconsistent: multi-view predictions come in short windows with independent scales and frames, while monocular depth is metric only per image. Our key insight is that, once reconciled into a single metric frame, these priors can replace dataset annotations for feed-forward 4D reconstruction. We present \textbf{FreeForward4D}, which calibrates overlapping prediction windows against monocular metric depth and registers them into one frame across time. On this geometry, 2D tracks are associated across cameras in 3D, and objects move through hierarchical motion that combines a shared object velocity with local Gaussian corrections, so that both training and inference use only RGB videos. On Waymo, FreeForward4D achieves 27.66 dB PSNR and 0.836 SSIM while recovering metric-scale cameras and depth without scale alignment. It surpasses STORM, SLARM-F, UFO, DGGT, and StreetForward, which rely on dataset annotations, in full-image PSNR and attains the best RGB fidelity on 8-second windows. RGB-only fine-tuning further adapts the framework to nuScenes and Argoverse 2. Code will be released.
