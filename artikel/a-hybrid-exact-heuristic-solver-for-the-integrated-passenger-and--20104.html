A Hybrid Exact-Heuristic Solver for the Integrated Passenger and Parcel Transportation Problem

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (185 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.22043v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We describe the solver we submitted to the second round of the SOICT 2026 contest on the Integrated Passenger and Parcel Transportation problem, a selective pickup-and-delivery routing problem with time windows, open routes and heterogeneous taxis. The solver is a single-file, standard-library Python program that combines (i) a constant-time insertion test based on latest-arrival slack, (ii) greedy insertion, local search and an Adaptive Large Neighborhood Search (ALNS) with simulated-annealing acceptance, and (iii) an exact route-selection engine that enumerates, for every taxi, the best feasible route for each request set and then solves the resulting set-packing problem by branch-and-bound strengthened with route-based large neighborhood search, Lagrangian bounds and reduced-cost fixing. The engine is chosen automatically from the instance size, and an independent verifier and a watchdog guarantee that a valid answer is always printed. On the official evaluation (38 scored instances, 5-minute limit) the solver obtained a total benefit of 487,237, finished every run within 277 s and used at most 279 MB; 28 of the 38 instances terminated well before the limit.
