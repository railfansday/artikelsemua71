Modular Hamiltonian and modular flow of a massive scalar in an interval

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (100 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.21739v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We obtain the vacuum modular Hamiltonian of a free massive scalar in an interval, for any $mL$. Its eigenvectors are superpositions of massive plane waves emitted from the two end points, with amplitudes given by a linear integral equation whose Neumann series is an expansion in reflections, and a normalization fixed by a Painlev\'e V transcendent. We follow the modular flow of initially localized wave packets inside the causal diamond. The flow is not geometric: the commutator profiles spread and can develop peaks ahead of the conformal trajectory.
