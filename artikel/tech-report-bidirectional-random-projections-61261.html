Tech Report: Bidirectional Random Projections

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (108 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2606.10377v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: This paper analyzes bidirectional random projections for ordinary least squares (OLS) regression under the fixed design setting. Let $(X, Y) \in \mathbb{R}^{n\times p}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a sample and $W \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1\times n}$, $R \in \mathbb{R}^{p\times p_1}$ be two random projections. We develop an expected excess loss bound for the OLS estimator built on $(WXR, WY)$, extending an established bound for the OLS estimator built on $(XR, Y)$. Our analysis shows that our bound is larger and the gap between the two bounds decays at a rate of approximately $O(1/n_1)$. These findings are confirmed by numerical results.
