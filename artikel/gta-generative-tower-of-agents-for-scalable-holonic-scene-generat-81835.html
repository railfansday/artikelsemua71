GTA: Generative Tower of Agents for Scalable Holonic Scene Generation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (138 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.23727v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Scalable world modeling requires coordinating scene-wide goals with fine-grained objects and dynamic characters under limited time and context budgets. We introduce GTA (Generative Tower of Agents), which represents every unit at every level of an adaptive scene hierarchy as a holon, both a self-contained construction task and a composable scene unit. Parallel agents recursively plan the hierarchy top-down, generate assets, and assemble and refine them bottom-up. Uniform interfaces carry scene-wide requirements down the hierarchy, guiding local construction so that each holon fits into the larger one. GTA accelerates scalable world modeling through high-throughput parallel execution while preserving well-structured layouts and fine-grained asset details. GTA then populates the assembled environments with dynamic human characters whose appearance and motion are guided by the scene, yielding realized 4D worlds. Project page: https://zt-zeng-tao.github.io/GTA/.
