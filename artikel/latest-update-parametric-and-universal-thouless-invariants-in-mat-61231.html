Latest Update: Parametric and Universal Thouless Invariants in Matrix Product States

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (108 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.21744v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate the classification of continuous families of translation-invariant injective matrix product states on an infinite chain, parametrized by a paracompact Hausdorff space $X$ and subject to a compact Hausdorff symmetry $G$. We construct an invariant for phases of such families, valued in $\check{H}^1(X;\widehat{G})$, that generalizes the known $\widehat{G}$ phase invariant for $G$-symmetric Thouless pumps. The phase invariant is constructed as the pullback to $X$ of a single universal \v{C}ech cohomology class on a classifying space of $G$-symmetric translation-invariant injective matrix product states. Several examples are given over various parameter spaces where this invariant is nontrivial.
