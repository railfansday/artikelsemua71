Wizard: Manipulate Any World in Real Time via Agentic Control

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (212 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2610.40339v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Recent advances in video world models have enabled increasingly realistic and interactive virtual environments, opening fresh possibilities for player-customizable gameplay. Agentic frameworks further advance this direction by decoupling explicit game logic and controllable world representations from visual generation. However, existing approaches support only simple game settings and predefined motion patterns, generalize poorly to unseen environments and characters, and offer limited real-time interactivity. In this paper, we introduce Wizard, an agentic game world model that enables real-time, precise control over character motions while generalizing zero-shot across novel scenes, characters, and actions. To achieve this, we construct a diverse dataset spanning 805 scenes and 178 characters, incorporating skeleton and geometry information as structured proxies to condition video generation. Our agentic framework explicitly tracks and updates game states to support character-aware adaptive masking and world-anchored spatial conditioning, improving character identity preservation and spatial consistency. Experiments on WBench show that our method achieves higher gameplay fidelity with comparable video quality and spatiotemporal consistency, while using fewer parameters and less training data than state-of-the-art world models. These advantages are also retained in our two-step real-time variant. Overall, Wizard combines agentic control with structured proxy-based generation to enable player-driven game creation with real-time, generalizable, and mechanically consistent world simulation.
