Signature Approach for Contextual Bandits with Nonlinear and Path-dependent Rewards: Regret Bound and Statistical Inference

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (183 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2605.10313v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We study contextual bandits with nonlinear and path-dependent rewards through a novel signature-transform-based approach. Leveraging the universal nonlinearity property of signatures, we approximate continuous path-dependent reward functionals by linear functionals in the signature space. This representation enables the use of efficient linear contextual bandit methods while preserving expressive sequential structure. Building on this framework, we propose \texttt{DisSigUCB}, a signature-based disjoint upper confidence bound (UCB) algorithm. Under boundedness and non-degeneracy assumptions, we establish a high-probability regret bound of order \(\tilde\mathcal{O}\bigl(d_{\text{Sig}} \sqrt{KT} + \ varepsilon_N T \sqrt{d_\Sig}\bigr)\) with \(K\) being the number of actions, \(d_{\text{Sig}} \) the signature feature dimension, and \(\varepsilon_N\) the finite-depth approximation error. We further introduce $\zeta$-\texttt{DisSigUCB}, a randomized variant that ensures persistent exploration for statistical inference. Under additional margin and long-run design conditions, we prove consistency and asymptotic normality of the action-specific ridge estimators. Both the synthetic experiments and the numerical studies on temperature sensor monitoring, sleep-stage classification, and hospital nurse staffing demonstrate the effectiveness of \texttt{DisSigUCB} over linear and kernelized contextual bandit baselines in nonlinear and path-dependent settings.
