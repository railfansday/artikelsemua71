Latest Update: InfuseLoop: Cross-Trajectory Latent Injection for Scalable Parallel Looped Transformers

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (212 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.22426v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Parallel Looped Transformers (PLTs) use otherwise idle decoding compute by overlapping recurrent iterations. However, vanilla PLTs scale poorly with depth: they exhibit training-loss spikes, and their quality gains shrink as more loops are added. The benefit of additional loops depends on the information available to each iteration, so we examine whether the existing computational dependencies limit effective depth scaling. We find that only the preceding in-trajectory state is passed across loop boundaries, even though completed cross-trajectory representations are already available. Based on this finding, we propose InfuseLoop, a cross-trajectory latent injection mechanism. It aggregates causally available hidden states from later prediction trajectories through lightweight attention residuals. This design preserves token-parallel training and concurrent loop execution, and requires only $O(Rd)$ additional hidden-state storage. Experiments show that InfuseLoop eliminates training-loss spikes and improves validation-loss scaling up to eight loops. At eight loops, InfuseLoop reduces perplexity by 3.4--17.1% relative to vanilla PLTs and by 10.5--37.3% relative to the non-looped baseline. It also improves average downstream accuracy by 0.84 and 1.99 percentage points over these two baselines. InfuseLoop incurs only 0.3% decoding time over vanilla PLTs and 23.3% over the non-looped baseline, while keeping the same logical KV-cache footprint as vanilla PLTs.
