JEPTERA: A Platform Agnostic JEPA-Assisted Model Predictive Planner for Urban UAV Navigation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (166 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.20593v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Urban navigation for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) requires planning that accounts for environmental geometry, vehicle dynamics, and moving obstacles. This paper presents JEPTERA, a navigation architecture that integrates a Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA), Model Predictive Control (MPC), PID control, and a safety layer. JEPA predicts the UAV's motion response to candidate commands for MPC action selection, while the safety layer checks trajectories against occupied space and predicted obstacle motion. Simulator adapters and platform-specific YAML configurations separate the planning core from simulator interfaces. An ablation study evaluated six configurations across three gym scenarios, totaling 180 runs. The complete architecture achieved the highest aggregate success rate, completing 28 of 30 runs. Additional tests completed one route across three city maps without collisions. Separate integration tests demonstrated operation in Gazebo/ROS 2, MuJoCo, and CARLA without modifying the core planning logic. These results support the feasibility of the integrated architecture for simulated urban UAV navigation and its portability across simulator interfaces.
