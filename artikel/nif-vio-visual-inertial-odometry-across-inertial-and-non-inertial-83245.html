NIF-VIO: Visual--Inertial Odometry Across Inertial and Non-Inertial Reference Frames

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (166 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.13662v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Conventional visual--inertial odometry (VIO) assumes that observations from cameras and an inertial measurement unit (IMU) are jointly consistent in an inertial reference frame. However, this assumption breaks down under non-inertial conditions. Inside accelerating or rotating carriers, visual observations of the interior reflect carrier-relative motion, while IMU measurements contain additional carrier-induced components, which can compromise conventional fusion. We therefore present NIF-VIO for localization relative to the local environment across inertial and non-inertial reference frames. It adapts visual--inertial fusion based on measurement consistency. As inertial conditions change, early constraint isolation is combined with local reoptimization to limit error propagation before inconsistency confirmation and repair the affected local geometry. Under non-inertial conditions, a compact model with temporally shared parameters is introduced to account for carrier motion while preserving inertial information that complements visual constraints. Experiments on public benchmarks and a self-collected dataset demonstrate that NIF-VIO achieves competitive performance under inertial conditions and improved localization accuracy and availability under non-inertial conditions.
