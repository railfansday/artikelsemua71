Industry News: AdaRSI: On-Demand Recursive Self-Improvement for Embodied Agents

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (181 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2610.20474v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Failures in long-horizon embodied tasks can arise from how a vision-language-action model (VLA) is called or from its execution capabilities. We introduce AdaRSI, an on-demand recursive self-improvement framework in which a coding agent first refines its tools and procedures, then adapts sub-steps that remain unreliable after testing alternative instructions and approaches. The agent collects demonstrations, tests their utility through temporary fine-tuning probes, and pools accepted data across tasks to train a specialist VLA that works alongside the original executor. At the end of each development round, its records are summarized into shared experience that guides subsequent diagnosis and data collection. On unseen composite tasks in RoboCasa365, AdaRSI raises success from 4.6% to 13.3%. On perturbed LIBERO-Pro 2.0 tasks, success rises from 32.9% to 49.1%. On RoboCasa365's Composite-Unseen suite, it achieves higher success than adapting every manipulation sub-step, using 34% of the GPU time and 36% of the demonstrations. On a real robot, the agent autonomously collects demonstrations and adapts the executor to recover fallen bottles, improving task completion.
