Breaking: Integrals of motion for the deformed $W$-algebra $W_{q,t}(D_l)$

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (145 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.10580v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: The deformed $W$-algebra $W_{q,t}(D_l)$ of type $D_l$ possesses an infinite family of commuting elements that can be viewed as a two-parameter deformation of the classical Hamiltonians for the KdV hierarchy of type $D_l$; these conserved charges are termed local integrals of motion (IMs). Concurrently, the quantum toroidal algebra $U_q(D_l^{"})$ of type $D_l$ admits four distinct representations via free field realizations, each corresponding to one of the four end nodes of the affine Dynkin diagram. Utilizing these representations, we associate an infinite set of commuting operators with each node, which we define as non-local integrals of motion. Both local and non-local integrals of motion act simultaneously on a shared Fock space consisting of $l+1$ free fields. We provide a direct, computational proof demonstrating that these local and non-local integrals of motion mutually commute.
