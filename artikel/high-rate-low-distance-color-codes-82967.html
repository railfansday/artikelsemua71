High-rate low-distance color codes

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (153 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.23420v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Experimental implementations of two-dimensional color codes encoding a single logical qubit have surpassed the fidelity of physical qubits. A natural next step is to investigate higher-rate codes that encode multiple logical qubits. Here we propose small, high-rate 2D color codes for this purpose. We design distance-$3$ codes with encoding rates up to $1/3$ and distance-$4$ codes with encoding rates up to $1/4$. We prove that these rates are optimal for color codes with only color boundaries on surfaces with non-negative Euler characteristic. We benchmark the codes by simulating Steane-style error correction with real-time feedback and no post-selection in a linear trapped-ion chain model with all-to-all connectivity. The two best-performing codes have parameters $[[20,4,4]]$ and $[[16,4,4]]$ and achieve break-even at two-qubit gate infidelities of $0.22\%$ and $0.26\%$, respectively. These results match the performance of Shor-style error correction with bivariate bicycle codes that use weight-$5$ measurements.
