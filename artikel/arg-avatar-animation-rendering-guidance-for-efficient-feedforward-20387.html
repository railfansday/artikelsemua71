ARG-Avatar: Animation-Rendering Guidance for Efficient Feedforward Avatar Reconstruction

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (189 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.22207v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Reconstructing animatable 3D human avatars from images is important for applications such as gaming and augmented reality. Feedforward methods make avatar reconstruction accessible by reducing inference time to a few seconds, but recent approaches rely heavily on scaling model capacity, training data, and computation. We ask whether such scaling is necessary. To this end, we introduce ARG-Avatar, an efficient feedforward framework with only 68M trainable parameters that achieves SOTA reconstruction quality. Our key insight is that 3D animation and rendering already provide strong geometric correspondences that existing methods largely attempt to learn from data. In light of this, we design the Foreground Avatar Coordinate RoPE attention (FACRoPE) and Intermediate Token Rendering (ITR) pipeline to guide avatar tokens to attend to their corresponding patches. Experiments across multiple datasets show that ARG-Avatar consistently outperforms prior methods when using two or more input images. Compared with LHM++, ARG-Avatar uses approximately 9$\times$ fewer trainable parameters, 4.8$\times$ fewer training sequences, and 14$\times$ less training compute, while running 6$\times$ faster and achieving a 2.7 dB PSNR improvement on the out-of-distribution NeuMan benchmark.
