EDA: Observe, Verify, and Assess for Evidence-Grounded Multimodal Depression Analysis

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (186 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22648v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Existing multimodal depression assessment methods often summarize long recordings into global behavioral descriptions. This leaves the source and decision relevance of individual observations difficult to inspect. We propose EDA, an evidence-grounded framework organized around Observe, Verify, and Assess. In Observe, literature-informed acoustic and visual cues are localized into source-linked Evidence Records with temporal intervals and behavioral measurements. In Verify, frozen multimodal agents examine whether the cited media support each observation. The simplified Support admission policy retains positively verified records and their review grounds. In Assess, statistical descriptors and temporal events support reference-based behavioral comparison, while PHQ-9-guided text analysis provides complementary symptom evidence. Cross-modal disagreement triggers review of the original text before final selection. Experiments on D-Vlog and LMVD evaluate human-referenced cue agreement and downstream assessment utility separately. AI cue annotations achieve 91.02% accuracy against human consensus labels on shared source segments. With a frozen 7B backbone, EDA Support improves F1 over direct prediction by 7.61 and 9.88 percentage points on D-Vlog Leave-Query-Out and LMVD, respectively. These findings support explicit, source-linked behavioral evidence for inspectable multimodal depression assessment.
