Information geometry and entanglement under phase-space deformation through nonsymplectic congruence transformation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (206 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2505.02269v5 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We investigate the relationship between Fisher-Rao (FR) information geometry and Gaussian quantum correlations under nonsymplectic phase-space transformations, with particular emphasis on noncommutative (NC) deformation implemented through the Bopp shift. Using the established affine invariance of the FR-metric, we examine the transformation properties of Gaussian covariance matrices and the positive partial transpose (PPT) criterion under invertible congruence transformations. For a bipartite Gaussian system, we demonstrate that keeping the canonical partial-transpose (PT) matrix fixed in the deformed phase space produces deformation-dependent spectral shifts that may cross the conventional PPT threshold. When the PT operation is transported together with the covariance and commutation matrices, the physical PPT spectrum and separability classification remain invariant under local Darboux transformations. We further investigate a two-dimensional anisotropic oscillator in NC phase space, where the deformation parameters modify the effective canonical Hamiltonian and consequently influence the Gaussian ground-state correlations and entanglement between canonical modes. The corresponding FR-geometry reveals that the original covariance manifold possesses constant negative scalar curvature. In contrast, the pure symmetric normal-form covariance family is intrinsically one-dimensional and flat. These results distinguish the statistical geometry of Gaussian covariance matrices from the physical quantum correlations determined by the Hamiltonian, commutation structure, and subsystem decomposition.
