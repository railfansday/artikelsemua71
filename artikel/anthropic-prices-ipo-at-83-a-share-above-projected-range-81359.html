📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic has priced its long-awaited IPO at $83 per share, above its initial range of $70 to $78, signaling strong investor appetite for one of the companies at the center of the AI boom, despite market jitters following the departure of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, which shook confidence in the industry. The Claude maker sold 980 million shares in the offering, up from the 825 million initially planned, generating $81.3 billion in proceeds and surpassing SpaceX’s $75 billion record to become the largest IPO in history by proceeds raised.

At the IPO price, Anthropic is valued at roughly $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, a significant increase from the $965 billion the company reached in May when it raised $65 billion in its last private market round, but below the $2 trillion it was reportedly aiming for several months ago.

Anthropic will begin its life as a public tech giant on Thursday, trading on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol “ANT.” A strong first day of trading would signal that investors remain optimistic about AI adoption, despite recent volatility in AI-driven stocks.

The company’s performance is expected to set expectations for private AI companies, including Anthropic’s main rival OpenAI, which was last reportedly valued at $1.25 billion but has yet to announce aims for an IPO.

Although none of Anthropic’s venture capital investors holds more than a 5% stake in the organization, the IPO is a massive windfall for its numerous backers. Investors who own approximately 1% of the company reportedly include Sequoia, as well as former Google CEO Eric Schmidt. Singaporean sovereign wealth fund GIC also holds about a 1% stake in the company, according to a person familiar with the matter. Other backers set to make billions are Iconiq, Menlo Ventures, Coatue, and Sound Ventures, a firm introduced by Guy Oseary and actor Ashton Kutcher. Spark Capital, the only VC firm known to hold a board seat at the company (it was the first institutional investor in the company, leading its $450 million Series C round in 2023 that valued the company at $4.5 billion), also stands to make a fortune off its early investment in Anthropic.

Some of the Anthropic’s existing investors, namely Sequoia, Iconiq, Lightspeed, Accel, and Altimeter Capital Management, are so enthusiastic about Anthropic’s future that they are reportedly planning to buy billions of dollars’ worth of the company’s shares in the IPO.

Industry observers note that yet the investors’ holdings in Anthropic pale in comparison to those of the tech giant’s strategic partners Amazon, which owns nearly 15% of the company, and Google, which holds almost 14%. A group of major tech companies, including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC Partners, have also agreed to purchase $26 billion worth of non-voting shares through a private placement alongside the IPO.

The firm’s seven co-founders, including CEO Dario Amodei, each sold nearly 3.4 million shares in the IPO, according to the S-1 filing, bringing in about $282 million for each.
