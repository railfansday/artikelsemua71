DAQ: Decision-Aligned Post-Training Quantization for Spiking Transformers

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (170 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22611v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: Spiking transformers combine attention-based representation learning with sparse spike communication, yet their weights and membrane variables still incur substantial storage and computation overhead. Post-training quantization (PTQ) offers a practical way to reduce these overheads, but preserving neuronal firing under weight and membrane quantization remains challenging. In this paper, we propose Decision-Aligned Post-Training Quantization (DAQ), a method that calibrates the quantizers to align neuronal firing decisions with those of a floating-point reference. We analyze how errors from these quantizers affect firing at decision boundaries and how they propagate over time steps in recurrent neurons. Based on this analysis, we formulate an objective that encourages quantized membrane variables to remain within the reference firing regions along the quantized model's own state trajectory. Experiments across multiple spiking transformer architectures on image classification, semantic segmentation, and event-based tracking demonstrate the effectiveness of DAQ with 4-bit weights and membrane variables. On FE108, DAQ achieves 58.8\% AUC and 90.9\% PR with SDTrack, while reducing energy by 66.2\%.
