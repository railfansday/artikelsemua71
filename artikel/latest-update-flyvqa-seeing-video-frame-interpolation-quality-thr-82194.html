Latest Update: FlyVQA: Seeing Video Frame Interpolation Quality through Fly Eyes

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (182 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.24012v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The ultimate ambition of visual quality assessment is to reproduce the neural computations of human vision and thereby achieve full alignment with human perception. However, the immense complexity of human visual circuitry makes this goal difficult to realize. Recently released fly visual connectomes provide a tractable biological starting point. Despite differences between fly and human vision, the fly's well-characterized motion pathways motivate us to investigate their potential for assessing the perceptual impact of temporal distortions. In this paper, we use a shared, frozen fly visual circuit to perform full-reference quality assessment of frame-interpolated videos, whose artifacts can disrupt temporal consistency. Specifically, compound-eye sampling maps reference and distorted sequences into the circuit, while a connectome-aware readout quantifies discrepancies in cell-type-specific activity and excitatory--inhibitory synaptic dynamics. Reference-guided temporal pooling and a lightweight regression head integrate these differences into a quality score. Experiments across multiple interpolation methods demonstrate better agreement with human ratings than CNN, RNN, and Transformer baselines with comparable parameters, supporting a biologically grounded approach to perceptual quality modeling.
