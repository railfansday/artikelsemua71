Cross3DGS: Unified Vulkan-Based 3D Gaussian Splatting Training from Workstations to Mobile Devices

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (232 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22240v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: Scenes captured with handheld and wearable cameras are still shipped to a CUDA workstation for 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) training. Training on the device that captured them, or on its companion phone or laptop, requires more than translating kernels: GPUs and drivers differ in parallel features, memory limits, and command submission, and these differences decide whether a training schedule executes and returns the correct enhancement. We present Cross3DGS, a cross-vendor, cross-platform Vulkan trainer that keeps one optimizer and chooses its execution schedule per device in two steps: rules on device limits, workload size, and observed driver failures exclude the schedules known not to run correctly, and a fixed preference order selects one of the remaining feasible schedules. The same core completes standard-quality training (30k updates, one million Gaussians) on every configuration we measured, spanning NVIDIA, Intel, Arm, Qualcomm, AMD, and Apple GPUs and including Android devices and an iPhone, whereas the existing portable trainers VkSplat and Brush do not run on some of the mobile GPUs. Averaged over 11 scenes on a workstation, Cross3DGS has higher quality than VkSplat with no significant difference in training time, and Brush reports about three times the training time at comparable quality. The final quality of Cross3DGS is nearly identical across devices. A mobile application turns a handheld capture into a viewable model entirely on the device.
