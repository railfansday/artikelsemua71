Time-Varying Medium from an Ensemble of Laser-Dressed Quantum Emitters

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (125 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.24861v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We propose a minimal quantum-mechanical model of a frequency dispersive, time-modulated medium that explicitly incorporates the pumping mechanism. It consists of a homogeneous ensemble of quantum emitters dressed by a pump laser. We analyze their dispersive, time-varying response to a perturbative probe field by deriving the macroscopic polarizability of the ensemble, and then solving Maxwell's equations via the Floquet formalism. This yields a rich band structure that departs from conventional analyses in which the modulation source is neglected, arising from the interplay between the dispersive polaritonic and time-varying responses. These results shed light on the role of the modulation source in time-varying media and pave the way toward realizing dispersive time-varying media from driven quantum-mechanical systems.
