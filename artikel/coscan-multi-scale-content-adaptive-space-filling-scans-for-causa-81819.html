CoScan: Multi-Scale Content-Adaptive Space-Filling Scans for Causal State-Space Image Restoration

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (194 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.23717v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Selective state-space models (SSMs) have recently shown strong potential for efficient image restoration. A central difficulty, however, is that a 2D feature map typically needs to be linearized into a 1D token sequence for causal state-space updates, making restoration performance highly sensitive to the imposed scan order. Existing deterministic scans follow space-filling principles but remain fixed and content-agnostic, and can yield fragmented or semantically inconsistent contexts under degradations. We propose \textbf{CoScan}, a \textbf{co}ntent-adaptive \textbf{scan}-order design for causal vision SSMs. CoScan learns image-dependent space-filling traversals via differentiable proxy supervision driven by \textbf{scan-quality objectives} that \textit{encourage short-range coherence and causal predictability, while preserving spatial adjacency}. To support hierarchical restoration backbones, we introduce an \textbf{efficient multi-scale scan construction by coarsening a fine-level minimum spanning tree}, producing coherent scans across feature resolutions. In addition, a \textbf{frequency-guided start selection strategy} is designed to \textit{place informative structures early in the sequence prefix to strengthen causal context formation}. Plugged into existing Mamba-based backbones, CoScan yields consistent improvements across several restoration benchmarks with minimal overhead and indicates promising results on high-level vision tasks. Code will be released at https://github.com/dongw22/CoScan.
