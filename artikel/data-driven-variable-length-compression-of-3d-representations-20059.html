Data-Driven Variable-Length Compression of 3D Representations

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (91 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.21802v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Compressing 3D shapes is important for the efficiency of many applications, yet existing methods either cannot leverage dataset-level statistics or use a fixed compression ratio for all shapes. We propose a learned variable-length compression method that exploits structure across a training dataset while adapting compressed representation size to individual shape complexity using only fast feed-forward inference. Our method operates on general latent representations and improves rate-distortion for both meshes and signed distance fields. Code is available at https://github.com/voilalab/varlen-3D.
