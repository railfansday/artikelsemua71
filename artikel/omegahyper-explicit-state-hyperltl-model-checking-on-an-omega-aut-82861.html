OmegaHyper: Explicit-State HyperLTL Model Checking on an omega-Automaton Diet

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (142 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.24210v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: HyperLTL extends LTL with quantification over traces. AUtoHyper is the de facto explicit-state model checker for HyperLTL, complete for an arbitrary quantifier prefix: it eliminates quantifiers through products of the formula's omega-automaton with the systems, complementing on quantifier alternations. However, it suffers from state explosion, as it fully materializes input systems and intermediate automata. We present OmegaHyper, an overhaul of AutoHyper that keeps its algorithm but decomposes and reduces the size of the processed automata, and integrates the Kofola omega-automaton complementation tool. We compare OmegaHyper with existing explicit-state and symbolic HyperLTL model checkers, on 120 benchmarks from their published suites. OmegaHyper improves the performance of explicit-state HyperLTL model checking by up to orders of magnitude; bounded symbolic tools still reach further on violated instances with short counterexamples, as expected.
