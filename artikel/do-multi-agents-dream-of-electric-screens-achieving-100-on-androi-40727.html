Do Multi-Agents Dream of Electric Screens? Achieving 100% on AndroidWorld Through Task Decomposition

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (222 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2602.07787v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present Minitap's mobile-use, a multi-agent system for Android UI control, and evaluate it on the 116 tasks of AndroidWorld, on Android emulators in Minitap's cloud device infrastructure, real Pixel 7 phones from our device farm and the stock emulator. On fresh, held-out task instances (three pre-registered seeds never used during development), the final system reaches a pass@3 of 100.0% (95% CI 96.8-100.0); pass^3 is 94.0% (95% CI 89.7-97.4) and the mean success rate 98.0% (95% CI 96.0-99.7). Minitap decomposes each task across five LLM agents (planning, subgoal tracking, state capture, decision, execution) and deterministic nodes, with mechanisms that read typed text back from the device, verify the goal on device evidence and detect repeated failures. A study of the initial system measures what each part contributes: removing only the decomposition changes success by -26.1 [-35.6, -16.7] points, the mechanisms together add +11.7 [+2.2, +21.1] points over a single agent, and the decision agent is the one role that needs a frontier model. We then improve reliability with general changes, a few of them triggered by the goal's wording. On MobileMiniWoB++, a second benchmark never used during development, the final system improves on the initial one by 7.1 points (McNemar p = 0.035; 95% CI -0.5 to +14.7). Minitap is open source: https://github.com/minitap-ai/mobile-use.
