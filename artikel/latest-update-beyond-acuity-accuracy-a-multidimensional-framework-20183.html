Latest Update: Beyond Acuity Accuracy: A Multidimensional Framework for Evaluating Patient-Facing Emergency Triage AI Tools

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (153 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.22045v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: Patient-facing AI triage tools may support care-seeking and emergency department prioritization, but require evaluation beyond acuity accuracy alone. We developed SAFE-Triage, a framework integrating human review, automated metrics, LLM-as-judge evaluation, and simulation testing across clinical performance, patient interaction, and clinician-facing summary quality. We applied SAFE-Triage to 40 MIMIC-IV-derived emergency vignettes and a 10,030-case acuity analysis comparing prompted LLM triage (PLT) with rule-augmented prompted LLM triage (rPLT). PLT achieved higher emergency severity index (ESI) accuracy (57.5% vs 42.8%, $P < 0.001$), lower undertriage (13.1% vs 29.8%, $P < 0.001$), and higher clinician ratings across all domains. rPLT produced shorter and more stable outputs with higher automated readability scores. SAFE-Triage identified trade-offs between acuity accuracy, undertriage risk, communication quality, readability, and summary quality, supporting pre-deployment refinement and ongoing monitoring by showing where triage tools are efficient, need improvement, or need clinician oversight.
