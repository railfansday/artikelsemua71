A Generalizable Approach to Safety-Aware Robotic Behavior Planning with an Explicit Constraint Space

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (206 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.18026v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Large language and vision-language models have substantially advanced robotic behavior planning from natural-language instructions and visual observations. However, safe manipulation in tabletop environments remains challenging because the safety constraints required for a task depend on the surrounding scene and may not be explicitly specified in the instruction. We propose a safety-aware behavior planning framework based on an explicit Constraint Space that stores reusable textual safety rules. Rather than retrieving rules solely by semantic similarity, we formulate constraint selection as task- and environment-conditioned applicability learning. A lightweight query-conditioned dual-tower model identifies the constraints applicable to the current context, which are then provided together with the original task and image to a multimodal behavior planner. To handle incorrect constraint selections and previously unseen hazards, we introduce a multimodal Safety Inspector that evaluates generated primitive aims, reconstructs the complete applicable constraint set, and provides corrective planning feedback. Validated inspection feedback is further used for test-time adaptation of constraint applicability and parameter-efficient adaptation of the Safety Inspector through LoRA, while newly identified constraints are incorporated into the Constraint Space. The resulting framework integrates explicit safety knowledge representation,context-aware constraint selection, corrective safety inspection, and test-time knowledge adaptation for safety-aware robotic behavior planning.
