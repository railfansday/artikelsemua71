Tech Report: DetailScale-SR: Decoupling Restoration and Continuous Detail Control for Real-World Super-Resolution

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (185 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.24212v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Real-world image super-resolution often seeks the most detailed plausible reconstruction, although more detail is not always desirable. The same degraded image may require restrained restoration, faithful reconstruction, or enhanced visual detail according to user intent. We present DetailScale-SR, a preference-conditioned framework that continuously controls reconstructed detail for a fixed low-resolution input without altering the scene being restored. Rather than relying on post-processing or repeatedly rewriting prompts, it learns a continuous family of reconstructions while separating content recovery from detail preference. This enables a smooth transition from conservative restoration to richly textured results under consistent input and semantic constraints. Its controllable range also extends beyond conventional restoration: DetailScale-SR can enhance high-frequency structures and generate perceptually richer textures than the paired high-resolution reference. Text restoration is another distinct strength. For text-rich scenes, especially Chinese posters and signs, it recovers more legible and character-faithful text than prior generative SR methods under matched textual guidance. DetailScale-SR therefore unifies continuous detail control, beyond-reference enhancement, and reliable recovery of semantically notable content. Our model is available at https://modelscope.cn/models/DiffSynth-Studio/Qwen-Image-2.1-SR.
