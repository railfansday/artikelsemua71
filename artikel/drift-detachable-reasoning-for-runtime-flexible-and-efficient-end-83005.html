DRIFT: Detachable Reasoning for Runtime-Flexible and Efficient End-to-End Driving

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (243 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2611.24719v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: The integration of vision-language models (VLMs) into end-to-end (E2E) autonomous driving has demonstrated strong potential by providing scene description and reasoning capabilities. However, VLMs are computationally expensive, and switching language reasoning on and off at runtime without hurting closed-loop performance has not been demonstrated, which hinders practical adoption. To address these concerns, we propose DRIFT, an E2E autonomous driving framework that integrates a lightweight language model (LM) for visual question answering and action commentary. By efficiently projecting intermediate driving representations into the language embedding space, we demonstrate that a compact LM (<150M parameters) can achieve strong scene description and action commentary. Furthermore, we introduce a runtime-flexible planning module that is trained on surrogate reasoning features distilled from the LM and can be conditioned on either the LM or the surrogate reasoning features at inference. This design enables bypassing the LM entirely when required by deployment constraints, without sacrificing driving performance. With only 255M parameters, DRIFT reaches up to a Driving Score (DS) of 97.03 and a Success Rate of 89.39% (mean over three runs) on Bench2Drive by interleaving its LM and surrogate reasoning at a chosen reasoning rate. This is on par with the strongest non-language models while being far more compact than existing VLM-based models. DRIFT further achieves competitive results on the real-world NAVSIM and AlpaSim benchmarks at significantly lower computational cost than existing VLM-based approaches. Project page: https://drift-e2e.github.io/.
