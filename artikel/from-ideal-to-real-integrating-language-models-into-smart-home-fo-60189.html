From Ideal to Real: Integrating Language Models into Smart Home for Human-Centric IoT Safety and Security Fuzzing

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (151 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22457v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Integrating IoT devices into household systems offers enhanced control over living environments. However, as these devices become pervasive in critical sectors such as healthcare, transportation, and home automation, they expose inter-rule vulnerabilities that may lead to privacy breaches, data theft, and physical harm. Traditional fuzz testing methods, which often operate in simulated environments assuming ideal human decisions, fail to account for the complexities of human interactions that can influence device operations in smart homes, potentially leading to unforeseen consequences. To reveal the impact of real human decisions and discover unexpected inter-rule vulnerabilities, we introduce an enhanced version of IoT Fuzz for smart homes by integrating large language models to emulate human decision-making processes. Our novel approach adds a layer of realism and unpredictability to the testing environment, more accurately capturing the diverse and dynamic nature of human interactions in smart home settings.
