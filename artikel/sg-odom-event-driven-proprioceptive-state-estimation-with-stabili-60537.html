SG-ODOM: Event-Driven Proprioceptive State Estimation with Stability-Gated Contact Updates for Legged Robots

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (189 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.20710v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Reliable proprioceptive state estimation for legged robots fuses inertial sensing with kinematic sensing from leg encoders. The latter assumes a foot is stationary during the stance phase; however, physical foot touchdown does not necessarily provide a stationary reference for odometry, particularly during impact and load transfer. Repeatedly applying kinematic corrections throughout the same contact phase can introduce redundant or unreliable information. We present SG-ODOM, a proprioceptive odometry framework that uses stability-gated contact selection to schedule kinematic updates. Following physical touchdown, a stability gate accepts the contact for state estimation only after the foot reaches a sufficiently stable support condition. The resulting validated touchdown event triggers an estimator correction, while additional timeout updates bound the maximum duration of uninterrupted IMU propagation. The framework is evaluated on multiple legged platforms, both quadrupedal and humanoid robots, against representative state-of-the-art proprioceptive estimators. Across the evaluated sequences, SG-ODOM demonstrates competitive or superior estimation accuracy, reducing Relative Pose Error (RPE) by up to 25% compared to the baseline estimator on outdoor sequences from the GrandTour dataset. Source code: https://github.com/ori-drs/SG-ODOM. Video: https://youtu.be/-583_RyuhqQ.
