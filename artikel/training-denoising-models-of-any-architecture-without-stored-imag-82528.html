Training Denoising Models of Any Architecture Without Stored Images

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (164 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.24223v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Can a pretrained denoising model replace the dataset used to train it? We investigate this question through teacher-supervised training, showing for the first time that continuous-time diffusion and flow models of any architecture and size can be trained from random initialization without stored images. Our students regress the teacher's velocity on a rolling pool of noisy samples, advanced along trajectories of random step counts to obtain independent, dense and approximately continuous noise levels. This saves considerable storage for large datasets and applies even when the data are unreleased or private. Our experiments include 12 teachers of 6 families and 6 datasets. Our method outperforms standard training of the same network on CIFAR-10 for students 4x smaller than the teacher, comes within 0.75 to 1.92 FID of it at the teacher's size and surpasses training on teacher-generated images and step reduction methods without teacher-based initialization. Our code is available at https://github.com/mever-team/ddm.
