Supervised Hebbian Class Networks: Learning Without Backpropagation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (114 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.24314v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: This paper introduces Supervised Hebbian Class Networks as a method for training multi- layer perceptrons and convolutional networks without gradient calculations or backpropa- gation. Each neuron in a learned layer is assigned a class, while connections follow the usual fully connected or convolutional pattern. The method combines a class-based supervision rule shared across layers with a Lion optimizer adapted to Hebbian activity products. All layers train together throughout the run, and their activities provide class predictions di- rectly, without a separately trained classifier. The experiments test the method on MNIST with a multilayer perceptron and on CIFAR-10 with a convolutional networ.
