Pretty good state transfer in Grover walks on abelian Cayley graphs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (150 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2508.09711v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: In this paper, we study pretty good state transfer (PGST) between vertex states in Grover walks on graphs. Using Chebyshev polynomials, we obtain a spectral and number-theoretic characterization for the occurrence of PGST on general graphs and on Cayley graphs over finite abelian groups. This approach yields complete classifications of hypercubes, the prisms $K_m\square K_2$, and unitary Cayley graphs. In particular, the hypercube $Q_d$ exhibits PGST if and only if $d$ is odd, $d\equiv2\pmod4$, or $d\equiv8\pmod{16}$, extending the known result for only prime $d$ (Chan and Zhan~\cite{pgst1}). We show that the independent-set blow-up $G[\overline K_2]$ exhibits PGST if discriminant eigenvalues of $G$ are reciprocals of nonzero integers. Using techniques from algebraic number theory, we obtain several infinite families of graphs exhibiting PGST, including examples with rationally dependent spectral angles not covered by earlier constructions based on linear independence over $\mathbb Q$.
