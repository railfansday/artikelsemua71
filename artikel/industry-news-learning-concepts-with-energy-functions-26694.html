Industry News: Learning concepts with energy functions

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 07 Nov 2018 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

We’ve developed an energy-based model that can rapidly learn to identify and generate instances of concepts, such as near, above, between, closest, and furthest, expressed as sets of 2d points. Our model learns these concepts after only five demonstrations. We also show cross-domain transfer: we use concepts learned in a 2d particle environment to solve tasks on a 3-dimensional physics-based robot.
