StrideWorld: Benchmarking Scene Rigidity and Action Consistency in Interactive Video World Model

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (307 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.24408v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Interactive video world models turn user commands into the exploration of a generated scene, which requires the scene to keep its 3D structure as the camera moves and each action to keep a stable effect. Existing benchmarks mostly evaluate these properties against references fitted to the video under test, either a scene reconstructed from its own frames or a camera trajectory aligned to the command path with a free scale. Such references can absorb gradual scene deformation and changes in pace and route length, so these errors can pass undetected. We therefore introduce StrideWorld, a benchmark whose references are fixed before the motion they judge. It evaluates scene rigidity against the ground-truth geometry of the start view, and action consistency against the stride of each action, the camera motion of one command. The stride is measured on the model's first steps and then used to test the steps that follow, so each model is judged only against its own steps. Its measurements are validated on rendered videos with known camera motion, and its scores agree with human judgments. Across \nvideos{} videos generated by \nmodels{} open models from $140$ start views, all models but one deform their scenes as the camera moves, with offsets up to $7.3$ times those of a rigid rendering along the same camera path. Most models keep a steady pace when a single action is held. Along composed routes, several models often stall, and most completed routes end away from the positions predicted by each model's own strides. Only the two models that scale commands by the estimated scene depth bring most routes close to these positions. Fine-tuning two models on a few rendered clips removes most of their mid-route stalls. Code, data, and a leaderboard are publicly available at https://github.com/chuangwang123/strideworld.
