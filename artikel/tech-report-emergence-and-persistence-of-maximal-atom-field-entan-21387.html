Tech Report: Emergence and Persistence of Maximal Atom-Field Entanglement in the Jaynes-Cummings model

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (216 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.22211v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the emergence and persistence of maximal entanglement between a two-level atom and a single-mode quantized electromagnetic field inside a cavity, whose dynamics are governed by the Jaynes-Cummings Hamiltonian. Using the von Neumann entropy as a measure of entanglement, we establish a direct connection between entanglement, atomic coherence, and atomic population inversion. This establishment reveals that maximal atom-field entanglement is achieved when both the atomic population inversion and the $l_1$-norm of atomic coherence simultaneously vanish. By considering the atom initially in its excited state, we further demonstrate that the persistence of maximal atom-field entanglement strongly depends on the initial state of the field. For field states whose photon-number distributions have no two consecutive occupied photon-number levels, the local atomic coherence remains zero throughout the evolution. Consequently, complete collapse of the atomic population inversion for an extended period leads to persistent maximal atom-field entanglement. In contrast, for other field states, the interaction generates local atomic coherence, resulting in transient maximal entanglement. Our results demonstrate how the choice of the initial field state controls the temporal characteristics of entanglement in the Jaynes-Cummings system. The generation and persistence of maximal atom-field entanglement in such systems could be valuable for applications in quantum information processing.
