Coexisting orders and dipolar Luttinger liquids in $\mathbb{Z}_N$ quantum torus chain

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (200 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.22046v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We establish a unified ground-state phase diagram for the one-dimensional $\mathbb{Z}_N$ quantum torus chain, which is connected to dipolar cluster and dipolar Ising chains via noninvertible dualities. Combining tensor-network calculations with bosonization and perturbation theory, we establish a rich phase diagram governed by the local Hilbert-space dimension $N$ and its parity. We identify phases with coexisting $X$ and $Z$ long-range orders ($C$ phase), and dipolar Luttinger liquids (dLL$_{\rm I}$ and dLL$_{\rm II}$) separating coexisting phases from single-order phases. Gapless dipolar excitations coexist with long-range order in dLLs. Both the $C$ and dLL phases appear at $N \geq 5$, but only the $C$ phases persist as $N\to \infty$. The stability of the dLL phase is understood by an emergent Luttinger liquid theory, with Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless transitions out of it driven by competing umklapp and clock anisotropy perturbations. For odd $N \geq 5$, a third dLL phase (dLL$_{\rm III}$) emerges near $\theta = \pi$ due to the low-energy dynamics inside the exponentially degenerate ground-state manifold that maps onto an effective XXZ chain. Duality considerations suggest a possible gapless dipolar symmetry-protected topological phase and spontaneous dipole-symmetry breaking in dipolar cluster chains.
