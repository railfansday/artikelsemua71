GenRF: Generative Registration-free Image Fusion

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (195 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.24157v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multi-modal image fusion combines complementary observations to improve scene visibility and perception. However, in real-world acquisition, differences in sensing principles and configurations introduce geometric discrepancies, while degradations further compromise source quality. Existing methods typically rely on explicit registration, yet large parallax complicates correspondence estimation, while mismatched fields of view leave some regions without cross-modal counterparts. Meanwhile, independent restoration underuses cross-modal information, whereas restoration-fusion methods assuming alignment remain vulnerable to misregistration. This study revisits misaligned and degraded image fusion in the era of generative foundation models, in which cross-attention enables position-agnostic complementary information aggregation, while generative priors support degradation recovery and inference in missing regions. Building on this perspective, we propose GenRF, which adapts pretrained FLUX through LoRA to jointly enhance degraded observations, infer missing cross-modal context, and fuse complementary information without explicitly estimating deformation fields. Extensive experiments on real-world infrared and visible captures show that GenRF delivers high-quality fusion directly from unregistered, degraded images without preprocessing, while improving downstream task performance under misalignment. Additional experiments demonstrate that GenRF can generalize to misaligned multi-exposure and multi-focus image fusion without task-specific fine-tuning. Project: https://linfeng-tang.github.io/GenRF.
