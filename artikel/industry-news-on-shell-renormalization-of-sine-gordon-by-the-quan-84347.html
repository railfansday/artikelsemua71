Industry News: On-shell renormalization of sine-Gordon by the quantum inverse scattering method

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (223 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2610.01571v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: In the quantum inverse scattering method the sine-Gordon model is solved on a lattice. Sklyanin, Takhtajan and Faddeev observed that the continuum and infinite-volume limits of the vacuum quasiparticle density become uniform only after the length of the box is rescaled, which absorbs the dependence on the cut-off up to a finite ambiguity in the mass scale. We develop this obser- vation into a renormalization of the spectrum internal to the method: since all the eigenvalues of the monodromy operator depend on the lattice only through the vacuum density, the uniform limit removes the cut-off, and an on-shell condition on the lightest breather fixes the remaining ambiguity. We show explicitly that the results coincide with those of normal ordering and of conformal perturbation theory: the dependence on the cut-off is the multiplicative renormaliza- tion of the cosine, with the exponent fixed by its scaling dimension, the masses scale with the bare coupling with the same power, and the spectrum agrees with the semiclassical one and with the exact S-matrix. Since the procedure requires neither a free field nor a conformal point, it provides an alternative, non-perturbative route to renormalization, internal to the quantum in- verse scattering method, which applies wherever an ultralocal lattice regularization is available, including asymptotically free sigma models.
