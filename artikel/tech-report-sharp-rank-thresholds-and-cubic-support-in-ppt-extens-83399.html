Tech Report: Sharp rank thresholds and cubic support in PPT extensions of completely symmetric states

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (173 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24500v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: For completely symmetric states on $d\ge3$ parties of actual local rank $N\ge3$, the least rank of an entangled state passing any prescribed finite positive level of the PPT extension hierarchy is $3d+N-3$. At equality, the two- and three-party marginals recover a ternary plane cubic shared by every extension. On this cubic the hierarchy becomes exact at a finite level precisely when every singularity outside the zero locus of the contraction quadratic is a split real node. We give the first exact level or an exact feasibility test for it, independent of $d$. On a smooth cubic the thresholds along the specified paths to the square of a flex tangent reduce to elliptic division and have unique rank-two Gram matrices. On reflection-invariant paths we obtain a quartic formula and the leading asymptotic constant. The least rank for entanglement itself is $3d+N-5$, and we classify equality. An explicit rational rank-nine three-qutrit state on a smooth cubic passes the first extension level and fails the second.
