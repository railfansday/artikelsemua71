PVTSizing 2.0: A Fast and Reliable Analog Circuit Sizing Operator for Agentic EDA Workflows

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (181 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22328v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Analog circuit design automation requires both coordinated tool execution and efficient numerical search. Large language models (LLMs) offer newly introduced ways to organize tool execution, but finding one sizing that satisfies specifications across process, voltage, and temperature (PVT) corners remains costly. PVTSizing 2.0 addresses these needs through a human-agent workflow built around an independently usable numerical optimizer. An agent carries the designer's approved task through execution and result review. The optimizer reduces unnecessary training through loss-guided updates and uses critic-guided local refinement to explore additional candidates while accounting for cross-corner conflicts. An optional LLM adviser supplies parameter relationships to focus numerical search on circuit-informed directions, while the optimizer selects adjustment amplitudes and verifies their effects through simulation. Across four circuit benchmarks, the standalone optimizer completes all 40 runs and achieves a 7.3x speedup in overall mean runtime over the original PVTSizing, with a comparable mean sample count. In a separate study, the best tested knowledge-injection configuration further reduces mean runtime by 39.9% and simulation samples by 35.4% relative to the standalone optimizer.
