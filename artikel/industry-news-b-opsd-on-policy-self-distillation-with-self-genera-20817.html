Industry News: B-OPSD: On-Policy Self-Distillation with Self-Generated Problem Briefs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (154 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.19273v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) has recently attracted interest because it provides dense teacher guidance without a separate, often larger teacher model. However, OPSD conditions its teacher on ground-truth solutions, which limits its use when such solutions are unavailable. We propose On-Policy Self-Distillation with Self-Generated Problem Briefs (B-OPSD). Using a prompt template, the initial model or a saved student checkpoint generates a problem brief from the question alone. The prompt asks for relevant principles, applicability conditions, and likely pitfalls, but not a final answer or a complete solution. A frozen teacher uses the brief as privileged context to guide the current student's on-policy rollouts through token-level distribution matching. B-OPSD uses no ground-truth solutions during training. Results on AIME 2024, AIME 2025, and HMMT 2025 show that both B-OPSD variants achieve higher average accuracy than OPSD at each of three Qwen3 model sizes. Code is available at https://github.com/ZevineXu/B-OPSD.
