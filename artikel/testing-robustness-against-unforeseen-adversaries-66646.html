Testing robustness against unforeseen adversaries

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 22 Aug 2019 • ⏱️ 1 min read (68 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that we’ve developed a method to assess whether a neural network classifier can reliably defend against adversarial attacks not seen during training. Our method yields a newly introduced metric, UAR (Unforeseen Attack Robustness), which evaluates the robustness of a single model against an unanticipated attack, and highlights the need to measure performance across a more diverse range of unforeseen attacks.
