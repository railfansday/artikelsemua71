Photonic Maxwells demon as an energy resource

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (198 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.21972v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We propose a new realization of Maxwells demon for photons and its use as an energy resource. The starting point is a multilayer dielectric mirror that transmits photons within a certain wavelength band but reflects photons at longer wavelengths, followed by a material that irreversibly converts the wavelength. A 3D box constructed from these one-way mirrors is a self-acting Maxwells demon for photons. The first case considered is a one-way mirror for visible photons, converting blue to red as an example. The next case is a one-way mirror that transmits visible light but reflects infrared light, followed by a perfect blackbody. We analyze a 3D box with such mirrors as walls to accumulate and store blackbody radiation. Applications include solar-powered thermal energy and solar-powered thermophotovoltaics. The latter case would combine the 3D box with an ultra-low bandgap semiconductor photovoltaic cell inside. In the ideal optical limit, radiation can enter the box but cannot return through the entrance channel after conversion, allowing repeated photon recycling and nearly complete optical utilization. This can in principle move the conversion efficiency toward the thermodynamic limit for solar-energy conversion, the Landsberg limit of 93.3%.
