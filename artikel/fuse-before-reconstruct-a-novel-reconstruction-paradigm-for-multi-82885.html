Fuse Before Reconstruct: A Novel Reconstruction Paradigm for Multimodal Industrial Anomaly Detection

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (217 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.24541v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Industrial anomaly detection plays a pivotal role in precision manufacturing, where joint analysis of multimodal data emerges as a critical approach to enhance detection accuracy. Feature reconstruction, a dominant paradigm for such joint analysis, is primarily implemented through intra-modal and cross-modal methods, both of which are plagued by significant limitations. Intra-modal reconstruction neglects complementary cross-modal information, while its cross-modal counterpart is prone to projection errors and ambiguous one-to-many mappings (e.g., same textures mapping to multiple geometries), leading to detection failures. To address these limitations, we propose a novel feature reconstruction paradigm, Fuse Before Reconstruct (FBR), which first fuses multimodal features and then performs reconstruction for anomaly detection. Firstly, we propose a bidirectional cross-modal attention mechanism to achieve deep feature complementarity between image and point cloud features, mitigating projection bias through channel alignment and dynamic spatial prior integration. Besides, a learnable pixel-wise gated fusion strategy is adopted to preserve multi-modal structural consistency while optimizing local feature combination. Finally, dual-path collaborative reconstruction is implemented to recover original modal features from the fused representations, where cross-modal consistency constraints effectively eliminate mapping ambiguities. Extensive experiments show that FBR achieves 98.3% I-AUROC on MVTec 3D-AD and 92.6% I-AUROC on Eyecandies, surpassing previous state-of-the-art methods by +2.9% and +4.1%, respectively.
