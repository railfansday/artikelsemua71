Improving mathematical reasoning with process supervision

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 31 May 2023 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, we’ve trained a model to achieve a new state-of-the-art in mathematical problem solving by rewarding each correct step of reasoning (“process supervision”) instead of simply rewarding the correct final answer (“outcome supervision”). In addition to boosting performance relative to outcome supervision, process supervision also has an crucial alignment benefit: it directly trains the model to produce a chain-of-thought that is endorsed by humans.
