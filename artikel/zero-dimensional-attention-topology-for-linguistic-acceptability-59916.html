Zero-Dimensional Attention Topology for Linguistic Acceptability

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (173 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.22312v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Topological summaries of Transformer attention provide sentence representations for linguistic acceptability classification. We examine a zero-dimensional persistent-homology ($H_0$) representation that summarizes minimum-spanning-tree merge values from 144 symmetrized BERT attention graphs into 1,440 features. The retrospective evaluation uses 8,517 downstream training sentences and 521 in-domain and 497 out-of-domain CoLA validation sentences after data-integrity filtering. The recorded out-of-domain Matthews correlation coefficients are 0.438 for the topological representation and 0.508 for attention statistics, compared with 0.545 for BERT. Attention-feature results are retained aggregate measurements; BERT predictions are rescored and length controls are refitted from the accompanying records. Analytical results show that the representation retains token-count information and discards a class of directional attention differences. Source resampling of BERT predictions also demonstrates the sensitivity of uncertainty estimates to the sampling unit. The reported results do not demonstrate a predictive advantage for this fixed topological representation over simpler attention summaries. They identify representation properties and evaluation controls relevant to subsequent studies of attention topology.
