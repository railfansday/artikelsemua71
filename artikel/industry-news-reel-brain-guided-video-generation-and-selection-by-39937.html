Industry News: REEL: Brain-Guided Video Generation and Selection by Reranking Encoder Embeddings with Linear Decoding from fMRI data

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (187 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.21907v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Visual brain decoding from functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) data has made remarkable progress in recent years, driven by increasingly powerful artificial intelligence (AI) models and the growing availability of large-scale public datasets. However, despite the impressive advances achieved in static image decoding, the reconstruction of videos from fMRI signals remains challenging due to the temporal mismatch between rapidly changing video stimuli and the slow dynamics of the blood-oxygen-level-dependent (BOLD) response. We evaluate a Bayesian-inspired proposal-and-rerank strategy on CC2017 and the BOLD Moments Dataset (BMD), using a linear semantic decoder, a pretrained video generator, and three neural encoding streams with ridge readouts and learned pooling in two streams. We refer to this framework as REEL (Reranking Encoder Embeddings with Linear decoding). Compared with a single generated candidate, reranking increases mean Video 50-way accuracy from 0.2359 to 0.2434 across three CC2017 participants and from 0.3653 to 0.3920 across ten BMD participants. These findings suggest an alternative and conceptually simpler strategy for improving video brain decoding, highlighting the potential of our approach as a complementary direction to increasingly complex pipelines.
