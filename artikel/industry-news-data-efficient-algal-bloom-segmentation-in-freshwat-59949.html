Industry News: Data-Efficient Algal Bloom Segmentation in Freshwater Bodies Using a Three-Stage Transfer Learning Pipeline with Attention-Enhanced Decoding

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (190 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22064v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: Harmful algal blooms threaten water quality and public health, yet automated segmentation of bloom regions in freshwater imagery is limited by scarce pixel-level annotations. We present a transfer-learning pipeline that adapts a Swin Transformer U-Net with SCSE decoder attention from ImageNet-1K to water-body segmentation (RIWA, LaRS) and then to algal blooms, together with a model-assisted annotation protocol that expands the labelled algae corpus from 145 to 655 images. In a single-seed ablation on a 41-image validation split (checkpoint S2-A4 and its variants), corpus expansion is the dominant factor (+11 mIoU points); water-body pretraining helps only on the expanded corpus (+1.1 points over ImageNet initialization); SCSE attention adds 0.3 points; and a learnable positional encoding lowers mIoU by 0.6 points and is omitted. Adding 476 images, 188 of them algae-free, reduces false-positive area on non-water scenes from 22.6% to 4.9%. Against nine baselines on a 256-image test split, a separately trained checkpoint of the same architecture (S2-A13) reaches mIoU 0.919 and the highest algae IoU (0.902); a near-duplicate audit indicates train/test overlap in this split, so these scores are likely optimistic.
