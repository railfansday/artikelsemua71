Hybrid Optimization-Generative Motion Planning for Multiagent Affine Formations

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (160 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.21264v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Formation maneuver control enables a multiagent system to cooperatively navigate through constrained environments in collective motion. While various formation controllers exist, they rely heavily on high-quality and dynamically feasible reference trajectories provided a priori, making formation motion planning (FMP) essential. To address the computational challenges for high-dimensional joint trajectories faced by conventional constrained optimization-based solutions, this paper introduces a hybrid optimization-generative framework with a focus on affine formations, which provide the necessary spatial flexibility. By formulating the planning task as a constrained Maximum-a-Posteriori estimation, we decouple multiagent objectives to leverage pre-trained single-agent diffusion priors for dynamic feasibility, obstacle avoidance, and smoothness. We then interleave the generative process with projected gradient descent (PGD) to strictly enforce affine geometric constraints with safety guarantees. Simulations demonstrate that our framework generates highly smooth, collision-free plans that are readily trackable by common distributed controllers and outperforms state-of-the-art planners in various metrics.
