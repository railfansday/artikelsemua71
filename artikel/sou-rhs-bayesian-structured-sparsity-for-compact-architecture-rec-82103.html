SoU-RHS: Bayesian Structured Sparsity for Compact Architecture Recovery in CNNs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (167 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24166v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: Highly parameterized convolutional neural networks often achieve strong predictive performance, but their memory, computation, and deployment costs motivate the search for methods that recover compact architectures from large reference models. We propose SoU-RHS, a Bayesian structured-sparsity procedure that augments Regularized Horseshoe Shrinkage (RHS) with the so-called Sources of Uncertainty Sparse Global-Local mechanism (SoU-SGL). The resulting model sharpens the separation between active and inactive filters, providing an effective mechanism for architecture recovery. A key property of SoU-RHS is stability: across different overparameterized starting widths and under a reasonable range of pruning thresholds, it recovers compact architectures with similar total size and layer-wise allocation. This supports the interpretation that the procedure is identifying a compact structure adapted to the data, rather than merely compressing each initial model separately. Across standard image-classification benchmarks, SoU-RHS recovers substantially smaller architectures than deterministic structured-sparsity baselines, while maintaining competitive accuracy, achieving lower negative log-likelihood in every evaluated configuration and generally lower expected calibration error.
