Reliable Evaluation of AI Agents for Investment Research: A Framework for Performance and Validity Assessment

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (208 words, 📖 Standard)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.22338v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We consider the problem of evaluating AI agents undertaking investment-research tasks, where agents interact with external information sources and computational tools to produce financial analyses and research reports. An notable challenge is that the quality of an agent's final output does not necessarily reflect the quality of its execution process, while the reliability of the resulting assessment also depends on the validity of the evaluation procedure. Furthermore, investment-research tasks differ considerably in their requirements for numerical accuracy, information retrieval, financial analysis and supporting evidence, making it difficult to assess agent performance using a uniform set of criteria. We propose a domain-oriented evaluation framework that distinguishes outcome quality from execution quality and accommodates these differences through task-specific criteria, deterministic validators and independent large language model judges. We also introduce an evaluation-validity mechanism that examines the integrity of evaluation inputs, reference information, supporting evidence and grading outputs, with expert review used to resolve identified deficiencies and significant grading disagreements. Numerical results show that the proposed framework can distinguish differences in agent performance across evaluation dimensions, while identifying potential reliability complications in the evaluation process. The associated implementation and evaluation materials will be made available on GitHub.
