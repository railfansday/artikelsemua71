Breaking: Cantor correlations II. Synchronicity and bisynchronicity

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (239 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22048v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: We study synchronous and bisynchronous no-signalling correlations, defined over Cantor topological spaces, from the perspectives of both continuous variable quantum information and operator algebra theories. We focus on strategies, satisfying a natural no-signalling condition across the Cantor space levels, called graded, identifying a universal C*-algebra, whose tracial states of different types characterise synchronous correlations of respective types. The universal C*-algebra for bisynchronous graded correlations is shown to coincide with the C*-algebra of a projective limit of free wreath products of quantum permutation groups, introduced by Bhowmick, Goswami and Skalski, and a type description, analogous to the one in the synchronous case, is provided. Extending results in the finite case, we show that bisynchronous graded correlations canonically give rise to factorisable maps on type I factors, defined via Cantor permutation matrices. We examine non-local games over Cantor topological spaces, with inputs/outputs that are finite words of equal length, define corresponding game algebras, and characterise the perfect strategies of the games via the respective game algebras. In the case of the Cantor graph isomorphism game, we provide equivalent descriptions of the existence of perfect strategies of quantum commuting type, in terms of bigalois correspondences, factorisable maps, Cantor permutation intertwiners and game algebra non-triviality. Our results supply a quantum information interpretation of a notion of a quantum isomorphism of locally finite graphs, introduced by Rollier and Vaes, canonically arising from Cantor graphs.
