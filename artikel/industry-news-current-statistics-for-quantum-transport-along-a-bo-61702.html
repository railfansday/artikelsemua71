Industry News: Current statistics for quantum transport along a bosonic tight-binding chain connected to Markovian reservoirs

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (99 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2609.11741v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We study quantum transport of non-interacting particles in a bosonic tight-binding chain coupled to Markovian reservoirs at its boundaries. Using the analytic solution in the form of an anti-normally ordered quantum characteristic function, we obtain exact probability distribution functions for the current on each bond in the chain, as well as the inter-bond current correlation functions. We extend the analysis to tight-binding chains subject to uniform on-site phase damping. In the phasedamped case, we find that a Gaussian approximation for the quantum characteristic functions predicts current fluctuations with good accuracy.
