A Study on Performance Enhancement of a Webcam-Based Real-Time Eye-Tracking System

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (167 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.21233v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: This study proposes a real-time gaze-tracking system that uses a standard RGB webcam to estimate screen coordinates. The system combines ETH-XGaze-based gaze estimation with nine-point calibration and polynomial ridge regression. To reduce errors caused by changes in head position, it also applies a conditional residual correction derived from head and pose features extracted with RTMPose. Whether this correction is used depends on the reliability of the detected features and the characteristics of the calibration data. In a preliminary experiment, one participant completed trials at 12 screen targets. The resulting mean screen-coordinate error was 204.24 pixels, which was 35.83% lower than that of xgaze-js and 57.69% lower than that of gaze_tracker. The system produced valid gaze estimates for all 12 targets. These preliminary findings demonstrate the feasibility of combining user-specific calibration with conditional pose-based correction for webcam gaze tracking. Because the evaluation involved only one participant, further experiments with more participants and a wider range of postures are required.
