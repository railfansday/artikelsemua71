Latest Update: End-to-End Landsat Land Surface Temperature Reconstruction with AlphaEarth Embeddings

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (184 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.24243v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Land surface temperature (LST) data at Landsat's 30 m resolution is important for many applications, but cloud contamination prevents its spatially continuous observations. Here, we propose A$^2$E$^2$, an end-to-end U-Net that integrates annual temperature cycle (ATC), coarse LST from Earth system modeling, and AlphaEarth embeddings for Landsat LST reconstruction. The ATC supplies a physics-guided thermal prior, the coarse LST provides a temporal background, and the U-Net converts the embedding field into day-specific fine-scale correction using observed pixels. Experimental results on four seasonally distributed Landsat 8/9 scenes over New York City in 2022 show that A$^2$E$^2$ achieves 0.78 K mean absolute error and 1.01 K root mean squared error, reducing error by 60.1% relative to baseline method without spatial correction and by 3.7% relative to the same U-Net guided by annual mean spectral reflectance. Sensitivity analysis reveals stable performance regardless of distance to clear pixels and percentage of blocked pixels. Independent validation with ECOSTRESS LST shows that Earth foundation model embeddings have better performance than annual mean spectral bands for LST reconstruction.
