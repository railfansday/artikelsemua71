The Walt Disney Manufacturer and OpenAI reach landmark agreement to bring beloved characters to Sora

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 11 Dec 2025 • ⏱️ 1 min read (48 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Disney and OpenAI have reached an agreement to bring more than 200 Disney, Marvel, Pixar and Star Wars characters to Sora for fan-inspired short videos. The agreement emphasizes responsible AI in entertainment and includes Disney’s firm-wide use of ChatGPT Enterprise and the OpenAI API.
