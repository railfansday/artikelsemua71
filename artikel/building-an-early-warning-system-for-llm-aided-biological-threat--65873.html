Building an early warning system for LLM-aided biological threat creation

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 31 Jan 2024 • ⏱️ 1 min read (76 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, we’re developing a blueprint for evaluating the risk that a large language model (LLM) could aid someone in creating a biological threat. In an evaluation involving both biology experts and students, we found that GPT-4 provides at most a mild uplift in biological threat creation accuracy. While this uplift is not large enough to be conclusive, our finding is a starting point for continued research and community deliberation.
