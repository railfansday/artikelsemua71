Joint Image Denoising and Interpolation via Mixed Graph Filtering

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (184 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24242v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Images often require both denoising and interpolation, yet treating the two tasks separately is suboptimal: interpolation introduces correlated noise that complicates subsequent denoising, while denoising may remove image details useful for interpolation. In this paper, we study joint image denoising and interpolation from a mixed graph filtering perspective, where denoising is modeled using undirected graph filters and interpolation using directed graph filters. We first establish that, under mild conditions, any (pseudo-)linear denoiser and interpolator can be interpreted as solution filters of maximum a posteriori (MAP) estimation problems regularized by undirected and directed graph smoothness priors, respectively. Guided by these results, we formulate graph-based joint denoising and interpolation problems using both directed and undirected graph structures. We characterize when joint denoising/interpolation reduces to a separable pipeline and when a genuinely coupled optimization is required. For the non-separable case, we recover sparse graph operators from given denoisers and interpolators, yielding an efficient linear-system formulation solvable via conjugate gradient. Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed joint framework consistently outperforms separate denoising and interpolation pipelines across multiple denoiser-interpolator combinations.
