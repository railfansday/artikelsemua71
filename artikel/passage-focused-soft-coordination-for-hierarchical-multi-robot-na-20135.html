Passage-Focused Soft Coordination for Hierarchical Multi-Robot Navigation in Bottleneck Environments

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (156 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.18877v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In multi-robot navigation, rigid global schedules are sensitive to execution deviations, while local methods lack long-horizon coordination. We propose a passage-focused framework combining soft global coordination with flexible local execution. A passage-induced graph of convex sets supports priority-ordered planning with two levels of soft coordination. Passage-aware route selection accounts for passage usage and traversal directions, while route-restricted continuous space-time planning penalizes temporal conflicts. The resulting references guide a model predictive path integral controller that avoids collisions online. Adaptive temporal and geometric tracking accommodates execution deviations, while selective replanning maintains coordination without rigid trajectory reservations. Across 1,200 instances in 4 different environments with 5-30 robots, the framework achieves a near-perfect success rate and outperforms evaluated baselines in makespan and flowtime. Hardware experiments with 8 robots demonstrate successful completion of all trials without collisions. These results highlight the effectiveness of passage-focused coordination in balancing long-horizon cooperation with flexible execution.
