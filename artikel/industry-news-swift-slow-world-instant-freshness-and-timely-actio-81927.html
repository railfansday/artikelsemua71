Industry News: SWIFT: Slow World, Instant Freshness, and Timely Action for Asynchronous Autonomous Driving

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (204 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.23813v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Driving scenes keep changing during model computation, making both observation freshness and action-delivery time central to world-action planning. To retain rich generative world priors in autonomous driving without stalling real-time control, we present SWIFT (Slow World, Instant Freshness, and Timely action), a deployment-oriented asynchronous world-action system that moves world updates off the observation-to-action critical path. On a background GPU, a slow Video DiT streams surround-view history into read-only predictive world context, trained with a predictive span that covers both the upgrade interval and background world computation. On a foreground GPU, a lightweight freshness carrier encodes current surround-view images into a gated key/value bank at every step, letting a fast Action DiT combine live visual feedback with the latest completed world context without waiting. On open-loop NAVSIM, SWIFT reaches 94.2 PDMS at 198.4 ms per decision with a trajectory scorer; on closed-loop Bench2Drive, it achieves an 80.30 Driving Score at 109.9 ms foreground latency. SWIFT further transfers to the Waymo E2E hidden test (8.0050 Rater Feedback Score) and nuScenes (0.54 m mean endpoint error, 0.03% mean collision rate). Across all four benchmarks, SWIFT achieves the best results among compared world-action methods, establishing a favorable accuracy-latency trade-off.
