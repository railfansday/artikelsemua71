Breaking: WiFi-based Global Localization in Large-Scale Environments Leveraging Structural Priors from osmAG

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (161 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2508.10144v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: WiFi localization offers a lightweight global prior for robots in GPS-denied environments, but fingerprinting requires dense surveys and generalizes poorly beyond surveyed areas. We model received signal strength indicator (RSSI) measurements using OpenStreetMap Area Graph (osmAG) structural priors. Offline, known anchor access points (APs) support sequential calibration of line-of-sight (LOS) propagation and wall attenuation; unknown AP locations are then estimated by alternating ray-wall intersection checks, signal compensation, and least-squares fitting. The resulting AP map supports online robot position estimation using the same structure-aware model. In experiments in an 11,025~m$^2$ multi-floor environment, our method reduces the mean AP localization error from 5.86~m to 3.79~m. Mean robot localization errors are 3.12~m in fingerprinted areas and 3.83~m in unfingerprinted areas, where four baselines yield 11.00--20.21~m. The WiFi-related map representation achieves approximately 251:1 compression relative to the raw fingerprint database. These results support structural modeling for compact global localization beyond surveyed regions. Code and data: https://github.com/XuMa369/osmag-wifi-localization.
