Breaking: Deep Mixture Models: A Statistical Review with R

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (186 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22149v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Deep mixture models extend finite mixture models through hierarchical latent factor structures with shared mixture parameters. This focused review examines deep Gaussian mixture models (DGMMs), robust deep mixture models (RDMMs), deep skew-$t$ mixture models (DStMMs), and mixed deep Gaussian mixture models (MDGMMs) within a common statistical framework. We compare their pathway distributions, measurement models, and latent architectures, highlighting the implications for likelihood evaluation and inference. For continuous measurements, we discuss Gaussian conditional calculations and the scale-mixture methods underlying Student-$t$ and skew-$t$ pathways. For mixed measurements, we consider response-specific likelihoods and numerical integration for discrete variables. Alongside the review, we introduce \texttt{MixedDeepMM}, an R package providing a unified interface for fitting and evaluating deep mixture models with Gaussian, Student-$t$, and skew-$t$ kernels, supporting continuous and mixed measurements and single- and two-head architectures. We illustrate its use on the UCI Wine and Cryotherapy datasets, reporting clustering measures with standard errors across repeated fits and visualising consensus partitions. For mixed data, Gower silhouette widths complement external clustering agreement measures. We conclude by discussing computational challenges, model selection, and uncertainty assessment.
