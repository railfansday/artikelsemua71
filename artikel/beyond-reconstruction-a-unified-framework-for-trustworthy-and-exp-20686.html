Beyond Reconstruction: A Unified Framework for Trustworthy and Explainable EEG Artifact Removal Evaluation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (259 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.21781v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Deep-learning methods have achieved strong performance in EEG artifact removal, but evaluation remains dominated by reconstruction metrics such as error, correlation, and signal-to-noise ratio. Although these metrics assess similarity to clean EEG, they do not establish whether models effectively suppress artifacts, preserve neural activity, or provide reliable explanations. To address these limitations, we propose TriX-EEG, a three-level framework for trustworthy and explainable EEG artifact removal. Level 1, Signal Validity (SV), evaluates reconstruction quality and neural band-power preservation. Level 2, Removal Faithfulness (RF), assesses the removed component through artifact error and residual artifact/neural leakage. Level 3, Explanation Reliability (ER), examines model behavior through temporal alignment, causal relevance, and stability. We evaluate TriX-EEG using nine published neural denoisers across three public benchmarks derived from EEGDenoiseNet and the PhysioNet Motion Artifact Contaminated EEG Dataset, covering EOG, EMG, and motion artifacts. Results show that reconstruction metrics alone are insufficient for trustworthy denoiser assessment: RF complements it by revealing whether errors stem from residual artifact or suppressed neural activity, while ER captures distinct aspects of explanation behavior, supporting joint evaluation of three aspects within a unified framework rather than reliance on any single factor. External validation on an independent EEG/EOG dataset and the BCI Competition IV-2a motor-imagery dataset further demonstrates broadly consistent rankings under dataset shift. Higher-ranked denoisers generally preserve more task-relevant information or cause less downstream performance degradation. Overall, TriX-EEG provides a comprehensive basis for EEG denoiser assessment, encouraging models that are not only accurate but also faithful and explainable.
