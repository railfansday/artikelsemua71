EgoLink: Egocentric Language-Vision Interactive Network Knowledge Challenge Summary

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (221 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.21690v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The EgoLink Challenge at ACM Multimedia 2026 benchmarks egocentric multimodal intelligence linking first-person perception, social reasoning, and interactive action. Unlike benchmarks centered on visible objects and activities, EgoLink evaluates whether models can interpret emotional and social cues, resolve visually grounded references, and convert user requests into verified tool-mediated actions. Its two tracks cover social reasoning in egocentric video and interactive agents that complete service tasks through dialogue, tool use, and closed-loop execution. Together, they test whether perception, reasoning, and decision-making remain coupled under both offline question answering and state-changing interaction. We highlight a multi-stage Track 1 data pipeline for multiple-choice question (MCQ) generation, chain-of-thought generation, automatic quality grading, and video-contribution filtering. We summarize the task design, evaluation metrics, rules, leaderboards, and representative solutions. The benchmark combines objective answer scoring with process- and result-based evaluation, enabling analysis of both final correctness and execution quality. The best Track 1 system achieved 0.9263 accuracy, while the best Track 2 system achieved 0.844 joint success and 0.916 micro accuracy. Participant reports reveal a shared principle: strong systems make intermediate evidence explicit through candidate verification, option-robust reasoning, scenario-aware planning, tool canonicalization, and state-aware correction before answering or acting. These findings highlight the importance of process verification and evidence-grounded reasoning for reliable egocentric multimodal intelligence.
