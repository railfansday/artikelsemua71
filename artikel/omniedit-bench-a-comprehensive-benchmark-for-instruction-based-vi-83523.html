OmniEdit-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Instruction-based Video Editing

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (163 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2608.05049v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Instruction-based video editing (IVE) offers diverse applications, yet evaluating editing models remains challenging. Existing benchmarks suffer from two key limitations: narrow task coverage inherited from image editing that overlooks video-specific dimensions, and metrics that inadequately capture instruction fidelity, allowing visually plausible but incorrect edits to receive high scores. To address these limitations, we introduce OmniEdit-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark covering spatial, temporal, audio, and reference-based editing. It distinguishes between explicit and implicit instructions and incorporates reasoning-based scenarios to reflect real-world editing requirements. We further propose an evaluation framework that assesses accuracy, preservation, realism, and consistency using both human judges and a state-of-the-art vision-language model (VLM). An accuracy-aware penalty mechanism conditions the other three scores on accuracy, prioritizing instruction fidelity and penalizing plausible but incorrect edits. Experiments on prominent open-source and commercial IVE models reveal substantial room for improvement. OmniEdit-Bench provides a structured testbed for identifying model limitations and advancing instruction-based video editing. Project page: https://omniedit-bench.github.io.
