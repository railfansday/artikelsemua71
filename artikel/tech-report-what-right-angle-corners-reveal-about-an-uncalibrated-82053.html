Tech Report: What Right-Angle Corners Reveal About an Uncalibrated Camera: Convex Cones, Certified Bounds and Identifiability

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (136 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.23934v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The corner of a box or a room, where three perpendicular edges meet, appears in an image as three segments meeting at a point. We ask what such junctions reveal about an uncalibrated camera's intrinsic parameters. Each junction is consistent with an explicit convex set of cameras; when only the focal length is unknown, this set is an interval in closed form. These sets are computed from the image alone, so calibrating from many junctions is a convex feasibility problem. Almost every junction bounds the focal length from below; we characterize when one also bounds it from above, and when many junctions determine it exactly. For a known vertex, the bounds can be certified against any bounded error in the measured edge directions.
