A Nonsecular Quantum Master Equation with Detailed Balance and Second-order Error Bound

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (183 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24911v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: An effective theory of open quantum systems should preserve the detailed balance structure inherited from microscopic reversibility and remain accurate throughout relaxation. Meeting both requirements is particularly challenging in quantum many-body systems, where dense spectra can invalidate the secular approximation even at weak coupling. Here we construct a nonsecular quantum master equation that satisfies time-reversal Kubo-Martin-Schwinger (TR-KMS) detailed balance. Under suitable conditions, we prove a time-uniform $O(\lambda^2)$ bound on the difference between the predicted and exact reduced density matrices, where $\lambda$ is the system-bath coupling strength. Our construction completes the Redfield generator by imposing detailed balance while matching its dissipative and coherent coefficients at resonance. In this approach, TR-KMS detailed balance is compatible with resonance matching, whereas its counterpart without time reversal is not. In a benchmark against the exact microscopic dynamics of two coupled oscillators, our equation maintains $O(\lambda^2)$ accuracy across secular and nonsecular regimes, outperforming the existing detailed-balance equations tested. These results provide a systematic route from microscopic models to thermodynamically consistent descriptions of open quantum dynamics beyond the secular approximation.
