Industry News: A closed-loop framework for perovskite precursor additive discovery

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (164 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2605.20242v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Efficient discovery of precursor additives is essential for improving the performance of perovskite solar cells, yet the large chemical space makes conventional trial-and-error screening inefficient. Here we show that LEAP (LLM-driven Exploration via Active Learning for Perovskites), a closed-loop framework combining a domain-specialized language model with active learning, prioritizes perovskite precursor additives under low-data conditions. The model extracts mechanism-relevant knowledge from the literature and converts it into interpretable molecular descriptors for uncertainty-aware Bayesian optimization. On an evaluation drawn from unseen literature, the specialized model provides more mechanism-consistent answers than the tested general-purpose models. In an expert-guided proof-of-concept study, two later-round additives increase average device power conversion efficiencies to 20.13% and 20.87%, compared with 19.25% for the control, and the best device reaches 21.32%. Because only a limited number of candidates are tested prospectively, these results provide preliminary evidence that literature-grounded molecular representations, Bayesian optimization, and expert assessment can support additive discovery for perovskite solar cells.
