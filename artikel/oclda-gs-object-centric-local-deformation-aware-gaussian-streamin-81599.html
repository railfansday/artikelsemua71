OCLDA-GS: Object-Centric Local Deformation-Aware Gaussian Streaming

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (203 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.23416v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Streaming 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) allows for online reconstruction of dynamic scenes from multi-view videos for Free Viewpoint Video (FVV). Current time and storage efficient methods rely on selecting a subset of anchor Gaussians and neighborhood interpolation for fast streamable 3DGS. However, these methods can overlook object-specific motion and introduce inconsistencies near object boundaries. We propose OCLDA-GS, an object-centric streaming framework that dynamically reallocates object and motion-aware Gaussian anchors to allocate anchor Gaussian support for objects undergoing large motion while at the same time, restricting the gaussian neighborhood within object groups for more precise object boundaries. Furthermore, it estimates object-wise rigid transformations (e.g. a hand moving) and leverages these estimates to learn per-anchor local deformation correction for small deformations (e.g. wrinkles on a shirt). Then, we conduct experiments on three dynamic multi-view video datasets and demonstrate improvements over the previous baselines alongside improved quality between refinement timesteps. Under matched refinement budgets, our method improves PSNR over Instant Gaussian Stream (IGS) by 0.169 dB on N3DV, 0.202 dB on Meeting Room and +0.199 on Google Immersive, while reducing the mean PSNR drop between refinement timesteps by 9.2% and 13.8% in N3DV and Meeting Room, respectively.
