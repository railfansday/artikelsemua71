Quantitative Theory for the Amplitude of Fluorescence Quantum Beats from Geminate Triplet-Pair Fusion

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (124 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2607.16461v2 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We derive the amplitude of fluorescence quantum beats caused by geminate triplet-exciton fusion from the full set of parameters that characterize singlet fission and triplet exciton dynamics. We apply the theory to tetracene and rubrene single crystals. We find that the amplitude depends on the fission time in tetracene, but does not do so in rubrene, where it is determined by the dimensionality of triplet transport. Kinetic Monte Carlo simulations reproduce the experimental data in both materials, for a fission time of the order of 200 ps and isotropic triplet transport in tetracene, and for a triplet hopping time along the herringbone axis of 200-300 ps and anisotropic transport in rubrene.
