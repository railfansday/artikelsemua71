From Data Quality to Data Trustworthiness in AI-Driven Software-Intensive Embedded Systems: A Systematic Review

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (162 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.23252v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: AI-driven software-intensive embedded systems (SIES) depend on data during development and operation. Poor data quality (DQ) may weaken data trustworthiness, yet evidence about its effects and lifecycle management remains fragmented. This systematic literature review synthesizes 27 primary studies published from January 2020 to February 2026. It examines DQ concerns and evidence linking them to reduced data trustworthiness, along with assessment and management methods. The review identifies 16 concerns organized as Source Quality and Content Quality. Studies provide DQ-based scores and evidence of effects on model or decision performance. Synthesis suggests upstream source or handling problems may become embedded in training baselines and remain difficult to detect at runtime. Eight method categories were identified. Runtime monitoring is the largest category, while upstream and training-time controls remain poorly integrated with runtime assurance. No study covers the full lifecycle. Data trustworthiness supports reliable SIES decisions but is one component of AI-system trustworthiness.
