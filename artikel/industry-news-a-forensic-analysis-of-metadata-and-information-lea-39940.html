Industry News: A Forensic Analysis of Metadata and Information Leakage in Swedish Public Documents

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (156 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.21922v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: This study investigates metadata and structural content leakage in publicly available PDF documents from 30 Swedish public-sector authorities. Using an automated collection pipeline and ExifTool-based extraction, 2,876 documents were analyzed and classified using an R0-R3 risk taxonomy. Results reveal that 48.4% of documents exhibit significant metadata exposure (R2 or R3), with significant variation across institutional tiers (p < 0.001). Longitudinal analysis across three legislative periods reveals a statistically significant reduction in metadata exposure between the baseline and post-enactment periods, yet 44.9% of documents published after the enactment of the Swedish Cybers\"akerhetslagen continue to leak sensitive metadata. Furthermore, 41.6% of documents contain incremental update layers; forensic analysis recovered verifiable text alterations, including deleted personal data and internal identifiers, from 228 of these cases. These findings highlight persistent regulatory lag and a systemic reliance on default, unsanitized document publication workflows within the Swedish public sector.
