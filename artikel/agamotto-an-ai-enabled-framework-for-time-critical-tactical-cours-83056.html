AGAMOTTO: An AI-Enabled Framework for Time-Critical Tactical Course-of-Action Evaluation and Generation

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (159 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24568v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Current command and control (C2) platforms lack integrated simulation capabilities that would allow commanders to dynamically explore courses of action (COAs) during live operations. This paper presents ongoing research and contributions to the development of AGAMOTTO (Advanced Guidance and Analytics for Military Operations, Tactical Threats, and Outcomes) as part of the overarching NGC2 program, which advances the Army's efforts to modernize C2 systems, such as the Tactical Assault Kit (TAK). The paper outlines the current design, preliminary findings, and the remaining research tasks, with an emphasis on how the completed work will support tactical decision-making in future time-constrained environments. While the full system is still under development, initial development and results demonstrate the feasibility of AGAMOTTO. Early experiments highlight both the potential benefits and practical constraints of deploying AI-enabled tools in operational settings, and introduce lessons learned and a viable training strategy to make AI-generated COAs feasible in tactical scenarios.
