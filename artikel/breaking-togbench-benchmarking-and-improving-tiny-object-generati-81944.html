Breaking: TOGBench: Benchmarking and Improving Tiny-Object Generation Fidelity in Aerial Imagery

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (133 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.23840v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Advances in deep generative models now enable the synthesis of realistic imagery. However, aerial imagery is dominated by tiny objects, such as vehicles, ships, and aircraft, which occupy only a few pixels owing to the overhead, wide-area nature of aerial imaging. Existing generative models struggle substantially more with tiny objects than with larger ones. This limitation has been overlooked, as prior work on generative evaluation emphasizes prompt following or human preference rather than whether small objects are rendered faithfully. To address this gap, we introduce TOGBench, a quantitative, object-level benchmark for evaluating tiny-object generation fidelity in aerial imagery. We further propose two simple train-/inference-time techniques that improve the ability of layout-to-image generators to render small objects, raising their performance on TOGBench.
