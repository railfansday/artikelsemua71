Latest Update: M-CROSS: A Multisensor Simulation Dataset for SLAM across Water--Land Transitions

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (168 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.19765v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Water--land cross-domain robots require continuous localization across land, transition and underwater phases, where sensor characteristics and availability change abruptly. Existing datasets and conventional metrics are not designed to capture transition-induced degradation and failures. We present \textbf{M-CROSS}, to the best of our knowledge, the first multisensor simulation dataset and benchmark for SLAM across continuous water--land transitions. It provides synchronized LiDAR, sonar, stereo camera, IMU, DVL, and depth measurements; 6DoF pose ground truth; sensor-wise medium-state annotations; and medium-dependent sensor degradation models. We establish a transition-aware protocol comprising the Trajectory Completion Rate (TCR), Piecewise Absolute Pose Error (PW-APE), Transition End Error (TEE), and Transition Survival Period (TSP), which measure trajectory completion, single-domain accuracy, transition-induced error, and localization survival under prolonged partial immersion, respectively. Experiments with 6 representative SLAM systems show that existing methods fail to maintain both localization accuracy and trajectory continuity across the full set of water--land transitions, exhibiting transition-induced drift, trajectory discontinuities, and tracking failures. M-CROSS is publicly available https://anonymous.4open.science/r/MCross-Dataset.
