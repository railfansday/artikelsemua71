Tech Report: Cross-Modal StateKV Injection for Communication in Multi-LLM Systems across Temporal Segments

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (181 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, arXiv:2611.22304v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: Video understanding with multiple multimodal language models requires a communication interface that can transfer complementary visual observations. Text-based communication is interpretable, but introduces an intermediate generation stage and can omit information relevant to subsequent reasoning. We study Cross-Modal StateKV Injection, a provider--carrier framework that communicates through key--value (KV) states at visual-token positions. Provider models observe complementary video frames; their visual KV states are aligned and projected into the carrier representation space and integrated through gated residual updates. Injection preserves the carrier KV-cache sequence length, while retaining the additional computation required by the providers and the communication module. The reported experiments on MLVU and Video-MME compare carrier-only inference, text-based communication, and StateKV with two provider architectures. Results show modest, task-dependent accuracy gains and lower reported latency than the evaluated text-communication baseline, with some categories remaining unchanged or decreasing. Increasing provider-side frame coverage offers additional gains on selected tasks without extending the carrier cache. These findings motivate further study of visual KV communication under explicitly controlled observation and computation budgets.
