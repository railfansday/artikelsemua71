Massive Brains, Tiny Board: Pupavision, an Agentic Physical AI Instrument for Monitoring Drosophila Pupariation and Lifecycle Transitions

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (297 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.22136v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: Many biology experiments are limited by the availability and ease of use of instruments that monitor and record them. For example, the timing of pupariation in the fruit fly Drosophila melanogaster, a widely used model organism, can provide biologically relevant information about growth regulation and is often recorded manually. Here, we present Pupavision, a small physical AI instrument built from a single-board Linux computer and a day/night camera for roughly 45 to 75 US dollars in parts. The key idea is that a frontier coding agent runs its agentic harness directly on the tiny board. The model thinks in the cloud, but the agent works on the device. The agent assists image capture, writes code to repair errors, and checks the experiment every day to keep raw data capture reliable. If recording stops, the researcher gets a ping on their mobile phone. A simple deterministic capture service records one image every ten minutes and never depends on the agent. We report a 26-day pilot in a fly incubator (about 3,580 frames), including eleven days in which the agent was logged out and capture continued, and a 51-hour outage whose cause led to the layered design. A researcher-in-the-loop workflow then produces per-vial pupariation curves by tracing selected pupae backward in time from a late reference frame. Future work will validate the assay for lifecycle tracking accuracy against expert-labeled data and expand real-time computer vision analysis. Pupavision offers a blueprint for do-it-yourself agentic instruments that put frontier intelligence on a tiny board, with deterministic code underneath. We present this as an early case study from a pilot lab deployment and contribute lessons learned toward a future where scientific domain experts are empowered by DIY agentic hardware.
