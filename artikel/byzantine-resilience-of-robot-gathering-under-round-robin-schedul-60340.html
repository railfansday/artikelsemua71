Byzantine Resilience of Robot Gathering under Round-Robin Scheduling

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (122 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.22631v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study deterministic gathering of $n$ anonymous, oblivious mobile robots in the plane under round-robin scheduling with rigid movement, in the presence of $f$ Byzantine robots. Robots observe all robot positions with their multiplicities, while their local coordinate systems may change adversarially between activations. The goal is for all correct robots to gather at a single point in finite time and remain there forever. We analyze ToMax, a simple algorithm that moves to a point of maximum multiplicity and stays still if and only if its current position is the unique maximum. We prove that ToMax achieves gathering whenever $f\le\lfloor\sqrt{n}/2\rfloor$, establishing an $\Omega(\sqrt{n})$ lower bound on the achievable Byzantine resilience.
