Breaking: Learning the Anyon Atlas

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (169 words, 📖 Standard)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.24752v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We extend our previous search for the modular data of topological quantum field theories in 2+1 dimensions from 22 to 42 anyons, identifying 150,679 new modular data. We do so by adding to our algorithm three rejection gates, two of them based on logistic regression, that discard representations of $SL(2,\mathbb{Z})$ unlikely to yield modular data. We estimate that roughly 80% of the modular data between ranks 23 and 42 has been identified after closing under known constructions. With this data, we find counterexamples to a conjecture of Geiko and Moore that the reality of the higher Gauss sums suffices for time-reversal symmetry in Chern-Simons theories, and propose a refined criterion. We demonstrate the scalability of this gated implementation by identifying prime modular data at rank 100 that is not realized by any Chern-Simons theory. The identified data through rank 42 has been made available at https://AnyonAtlas.org, to which further data will be added as it is found.
