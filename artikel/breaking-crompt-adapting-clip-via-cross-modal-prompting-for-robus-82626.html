Breaking: CROMPT: Adapting CLIP via Cross-Modal Prompting for Robust Zero-Shot Generalization under Adverse Weather

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (153 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24284v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Domain adaptation for semantic segmentation is challenging when target-domain images are unavailable during training. Zero-shot domain adaptation addresses this by adapting models using only textual descriptions of the target domain. However, existing prompt-learning methods are not well suited for ResNet-based CLIP vision backbones or pixel-level cross-modal feature alignment. We propose CROMPT, a prompt-learning framework for zero-shot domain adaptation in semantic segmentation. CROMPT introduces Cross-Modal Feature Augmentation (CROMA), combining Random Visual Prompting with Hybrid Textual Prompting (HTP) to jointly adapt visual and textual representations. We further propose CROMA-VL, which leverages vision-language models to generate domain-conditioned prompts, and a progressive easy-to-hard adaptation strategy for improved domain invariance. Experiments on two benchmark datasets demonstrate consistent improvements over strong baselines and state-of-the-art methods, achieving 1.63-2.63% mIoU gains while maintaining inference-time complexity. These results establish CROMPT as an effective and efficient framework for zero-shot multi-domain semantic segmentation. OpenReview: https://openreview.net/forum?id=FxqFsIL685.
