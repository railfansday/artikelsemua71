Tech Report: JET: Just-Enough Exploration Tuning for Efficient Tool-Calling Agents in Repository-Level Question Answering

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (207 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.22539v1 Announce Type: latest Abstract: In repository-level question answering (QA), an agent explores a code repository with tools and should answer a question once enough information is gathered. Nevertheless, we find that LLM agents often continue to invoke tools even after the gathered information is already sufficient to answer. We call this phenomenon over-exploration. Moreover, even though existing methods improve task performance through supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on expert trajectories, SFT also propagates the over-exploration in the trajectories to the agent. To mitigate over-exploration, we propose Just-Enough Exploration Tuning (JET), a fine-tuning method that trains the agent to answer once the gathered information is sufficient. JET truncates each training trajectory at the step where the gathered information becomes sufficient, inserts a rationale that explains why the agent can answer at that step, and fine-tunes the agent with a higher loss weight on the rationale. Experiments on two repository-level QA benchmarks with two open-weight 8B models demonstrate that JET reduces tool calls by 11 to 33% on self-generated trajectories while largely preserving the score. On expert trajectories, JET makes 18 to 28% fewer tool calls than SFT while retaining most of the score gain of SFT. In both cases, JET also lowers the inference cost.
