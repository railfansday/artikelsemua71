MicroAssert: Microarchitecture-Aware Structured Assertion Generation for CPU Verification

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (135 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2611.24872v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Automatic assertion generation reduces manual verification effort, yet existing approaches struggle to capture microarchitectural behaviors and cross-region interactions in CPUs. We present MicroAssert, a microarchitecture-aware framework that identifies functional regions directly from RTL and generates local and composite assertions through structured contracts and deterministic verification primitives. Coverage-guided feedback further improves verification completeness by progressively expanding proof-relevant RTL coverage. Evaluated on five open-source RISC-V CPUs against three existing assertion generation approaches, MicroAssert achieves 100% syntax correctness and complete branch, statement, and toggle coverage, with an average proven rate of 74.82% and Proof Core coverage of 90.96%. Mutation testing further demonstrates its advantage, achieving an average error detection rate of 28.73%, 1.99 to 3.51 times higher than existing methods. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of microarchitecture-aware assertion generation.
