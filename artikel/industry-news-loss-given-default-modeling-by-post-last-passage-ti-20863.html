Industry News: Loss-Given-Default Modeling by Post-Last Passage Time Process

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (120 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2009.00868v5 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We bridge the dichotomy between structural and intensity-based credit models by defining default via the last passage time ($\lambda_\alpha$) of a linear diffusion. Because $\lambda_\alpha$ is not a stopping time, this approach naturally generates "surprise" defaults within an asset-value framework without artificial filtration enlargements. Mathematically, we establish two general results for transient linear diffusions: we derive explicit Laplace transforms for occupation times and characterize post-passage dynamics. Practically, our two-parameter model calibrates seamlessly to default probabilities and Credit Default Swap (CDS) spreads. We obtain firm-specific Loss-Given-Default (LGD) densities - resolving a long-standing open question - and quantify sentiment divergence between equity and credit markets, yielding a unified valuation framework.
