H3Remover: Reinforcing Video Object Removal without Paired Data

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (151 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2611.24633v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Pretrained video editors possess rich removal and completion priors, yet may retain targets or alter unrelated content. We present H3Remover, which reinforces MiniMax-H3 Base Ref2VA for video object removal without paired object-free training targets. To discourage superficial edits such as recoloring, a target-removal reward favors calibrated depth changes consistent with revealing farther content. To limit unintended scene changes, a background-geometry reward encourages preservation of relative depth ordering. To preserve background appearance without retaining unwanted shadows or reflections, an appearance reward constrains RGB changes in source-derived protected regions while exempting targets and estimated effects. Frozen HPSv3 complements these constraints with perceptual-quality feedback for synthesized content. Evaluation regions remain fixed across candidates, and DiffusionART optimizes LoRA adapters with the backbone frozen. Experiments show substantial reconstruction gains over original H3, with qualitative comparisons demonstrating more effective object removal and better preservation of surrounding scene content.
