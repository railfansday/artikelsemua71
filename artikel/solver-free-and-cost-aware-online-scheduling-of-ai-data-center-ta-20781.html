Solver-Free and Cost-Aware Online Scheduling of AI Data Center Tasks

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (129 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.22239v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: AI data centers receive computing tasks continuously, whereas solver-based optimization takes time to run and electricity is priced per settlement period. This paper bridges this timing gap through a solver-free online optimization rule inspired by dual decomposition. The rule assigns each task a start period upon arrival to optimize energy cost subject to operational constraints, shifting flexible tasks toward low-price periods while keeping high-priority tasks on schedule. Simulations on the A100 GPU trace (3,456 GPUs, 1,343 tasks) show that the proposed rule keeps GPU and CPU use within capacity while scheduling tasks at low cost. Compared with a multi-timescale optimization benchmark, it achieves a lower cost and, because it needs no solver, much less computation time.
