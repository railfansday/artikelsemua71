Breaking: TPHC: Improving Hyper-Connections with Tensor-Parallel Grouped Updates

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (218 words, 📖 Standard)

Recent findings suggest that arXiv:2611.23026v1 Announce Type: fresh Abstract: Hyper-Connections (HC) expand the residual stream of a Transformer into $N$ parallel streams, and manifold-constrained HC (mHC) stabilizes them at scale. However, mHC benefits little from more than four streams, partly because each sublayer writes a single output to all streams, limiting how differently they can be updated. In this work, we propose TPHC, which integrates tensor-parallel (TP) shards with HC streams to promote greater independence across streams. Specifically, we route a separate mixture of the residual streams to each TP shard and keep the individual shard outputs separate during write-back, so each stream can receive a different mixture of those outputs. This design makes the residual-stream updates more expressive without adding parameters or FLOPs to the attention or FFN sublayers. With native support for stream sharding under TP, TPHC requires only 9% more training time than mHC on an 8B model when both use four streams. Increasing the number of streams from four to sixteen raises its training time by only 19%. On a 1.1B model trained on 100B tokens, TPHC reduces validation loss by 0.7-1.0% and raises average zero-shot accuracy across six commonsense reasoning benchmarks by 0.3-0.7 percentage points relative to mHC at matched stream counts $N\in\{4,8,16\}$. Our code is available at https://github.com/wuyou2002/TPHC.
