Renormalization and exact Bloch bands: the trigonometric Rosen-Morse model

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (124 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that arXiv:2611.23649v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study the trigonometric Rosen-Morse potential as an isolated singular well and as an exactly solvable periodic lattice. The potential combines inverse-square singularities with Coulomb terms of opposite signs. In the strongly attractive regime, independent renormalization at the two endpoints gives a two-parameter family of spectra. Deep negative-energy levels form two asymptotic sequences with the same Efimov-like scaling and opposite first-order Coulomb corrections. In the weak-medium regime, self-adjoint matching conditions allow transmission between neighboring cells. We obtain the exact Bloch dispersion relation and determine the band widths, gaps, and electron and hole effective masses. At fixed band index, the bandwidth becomes exponentially small in the limit of large Coulomb coupling.
