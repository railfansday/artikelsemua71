Industry News: QTOP: Scaling Humanoid Motion Tracking with Quantile Critics

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (158 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, arXiv:2611.20791v1 Announce Type: newly introduced Abstract: Learning human motion tracking policies through reinforcement learning (RL) has emerged as a core capability that underpins a majority of recent progress in humanoid whole-body control. However, achieving generalization to unseen motions at high fidelity requires jointly scaling training to thousands of GPU and dataset hours. This work hypothesizes that motion tracking is inherently compute-bound because training on a diverse set of motions introduces learning interference, similar to multi-task RL. To tackle this, we propose Quantile Tracking Optimization (QTOP), which improves sample efficiency by introducing a distributional advantage estimate conditioned on state and reference pairs. QTOP outperforms on and off-policy RL baselines both in simulation and real-world tracking scenarios on multiple datasets by up to 10\%. Furthermore, we establish a scaling law for QTOP and find that, aside from scaling predictably, dataset size has minimal impact on performance for moderately sized GPU hour regimes.
