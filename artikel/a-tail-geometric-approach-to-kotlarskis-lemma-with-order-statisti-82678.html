A Tail-Geometric Approach to Kotlarski's Lemma with Order Statistics

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (126 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that arXiv:2611.23642v1 Announce Type: upcoming Abstract: We develop a tail-geometric approach to nonparametric identification in repeated-measurement models based on large within-unit gaps. Differencing eliminates the common latent component, while conditioning on a large gap pushes the idiosyncratic errors into opposite tails. Under a regular thinner-than-Gaussian tail condition, an idiosyncratic component concentrates around a deterministic, gap-dependent location. Consequently, the corresponding observed measurement, after recentering, reveals the distribution of the common latent component up to location. The idiosyncratic distribution can then be recovered through deconvolution. This principle applies both to the classical two-measurement Kotlarski model and to settings where only order statistics are observed. Applications include auctions and wage-offer models, whether all offers are observed or only the top-ranked ones are available.
