Reimagine Video Dynamics: A Unified Framework for Video Editing with Editable Spatiotemporal Tokens

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (152 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that arXiv:2609.36496v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Most video editing methods can change how a scene looks but offer limited control over how it moves. We introduce Reimagine Video Dynamics (RVD), a unified framework for editing actions, motion pace, and appearance with reusable dynamics tokens and a shared renderer. The tokens capture motion from the source video and retain an explicit temporal axis. A language-guided editor transforms them to change an action or event. Resampling them changes the pace of motion, while keeping them fixed allows the renderer to reproduce the motion with a latest appearance. We train the renderer to reconstruct source videos. We pretrain the editor on motion from real videos, then fine-tune it on counterfactual pairs. Across 450 dynamics edits, RVD achieves the highest instruction-following score and an 80.0\% pass rate. The same renderer supports retiming and motion reuse without additional task-specific training.
