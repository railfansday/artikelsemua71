AI-written critiques help humans notice flaws

🌐 Source: Openai • 📅 13 Jun 2022 • ⏱️ 1 min read (60 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that we trained “critique-writing” models to describe flaws in summaries. Human evaluators find flaws in summaries much more often when shown our model’s critiques. Larger models are better at self-critiquing, with scale improving critique-writing more than summary-writing. This reveals promise for using AI systems to assist human supervision of AI systems on difficult tasks.
