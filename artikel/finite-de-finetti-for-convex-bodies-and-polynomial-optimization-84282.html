Finite de Finetti for convex bodies and Polynomial Optimization

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (176 words, 📖 Standard)

arXiv:2601.15184v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We study polynomial optimization over convex bodies with separately affine objectives and local affine equality and inequality constraints. We construct a convergent hierarchy of conic relaxations with explicit finite-level error bounds of order $n^{-1/2}$ at level $n$, complemented by a constructive rounding scheme that produces feasible points. The relaxation bounds and rounded objective values bracket the optimum at the same rate. The analytic ingredient is a finite de Finetti representation theorem for arbitrary proper cones: bipartite marginals of symmetric extensions in maximal tensor products are $O(n^{-1/2})$-close, in base norm, to points of the minimal tensor product. Its proof combines a uniform, monogamy-type bound on a variational mutual information based on Frenkel's relative entropy with classical conditioning and informationally complete measurements. We apply the framework to the assemblage relaxation of two-player non-local games in general probabilistic theories. For polytopic state spaces, the hierarchy consists of linear programs, and experiments on the Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt (CHSH) game provide a numerical proof of concept for the outer approximations and feasible rounding procedure.
